AfuChat is more than a messaging app — it's a **super-app**. The **Apps tab** at the bottom of your screen is a full-featured launcher housing 18+ mini-apps that cover everything from digital payments and AI assistance to shopping, gaming, freelancing, and beyond. Every mini-app runs natively inside AfuChat, so you never need to download a separate app or switch between platforms. Just tap, launch, and get things done.

## How mini-apps work

Mini-apps live entirely within AfuChat. When you open one, it loads instantly inside the app — no sign-in required and no separate installation. You can minimise any running mini-app and return to your chats, then resume exactly where you left off. Apps that support **keepAlive** (like AfuAI and AfuPay) stay active in the background so they respond faster the next time you open them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Apps tab">
    Tap the **Apps** icon in the bottom navigation bar of AfuChat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your app">
    Scroll through the launcher grid or use the search bar at the top to find the mini-app you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap to launch">
    Tap the app card to open it. The app loads instantly inside AfuChat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Minimise and resume">
    Swipe down or tap the back arrow to return to AfuChat. Tap the app again any time to resume right where you left off.
  </Step>
</Steps>
