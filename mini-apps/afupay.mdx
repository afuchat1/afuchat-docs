AfuPay is the digital wallet built directly into AfuChat. It lets you send money to any user, receive payments, top up your balance, request funds, and pay merchants by scanning a QR code — all without ever leaving the app. AfuPay uses two currencies: **ACoins** for real-value transactions and **Nexa** as a rewards currency you earn through activity on the platform.

## Your two currencies

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="ACoins" icon="coin">
    ACoins are AfuChat's in-app currency with real monetary value. **1 ACoin = $0.01 USD.** You use ACoins to send money, shop on AfuMarket, pay for services, tip creators, and more. Top up your ACoins balance at any time from the wallet screen.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Nexa" icon="star">
    Nexa are reward points you earn by being active on AfuChat — inviting friends via the Referral program, completing daily tasks, and participating in promotions. Use Nexa to unlock perks, boost posts, and access premium features.

</CardGroup>

## Sending ACoins

Sending money to another AfuChat user takes just a few taps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AfuPay">
    Tap the **Apps** tab, then tap **AfuPay** to open your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Send">
    On the wallet home screen, tap the **Send** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the recipient">
    Search by username, phone number, or scroll your contacts list to find the person you want to pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    Type the ACoin amount you want to send. The equivalent in your local currency is displayed below the field for reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note (optional)">
    Add a short note or emoji so the recipient knows what the payment is for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and send">
    Review the summary and tap **Confirm**. The ACoins are transferred instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Requesting money

If you're owed money, you can send a payment request directly inside AfuChat.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AfuPay and tap Request">
    From your wallet home screen, tap **Request**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who to request from">
    Search for the user or select them from your contacts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount and send the request">
    Specify the ACoin amount and an optional note, then tap **Send Request**. The other person receives a tap-to-pay notification in their chat with you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Scanning QR to pay

AfuPay supports QR-code payments for merchants and peer-to-peer transfers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Scan in AfuPay">
    On the wallet home screen, tap the **Scan** icon (or navigate to `afuchat://wallet/scan`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point your camera at the QR code">
    Align the QR code within the frame. AfuPay reads it automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm the payment">
    The payment details — recipient and amount — are pre-filled. Verify them and tap **Pay**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Topping up your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Top Up">
    From the wallet home screen, tap **Top Up**, or navigate directly to `afuchat://wallet/topup`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a top-up method">
    Select from available payment methods for your region — mobile money, card, or bank transfer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount and confirm">
    Enter how much you want to add and confirm the transaction. ACoins appear in your wallet within seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Transaction history

Every send, receive, top-up, and payment is logged in your **Transaction History**. Open AfuPay and scroll down (or tap the history icon) to see a timestamped list of all activity, including the counterparty name, amount, and status.

## Supported local currencies

AfuPay displays equivalent local-currency values to help you understand exactly what you're spending. Supported display currencies include:

| Country | Currency |

| Uganda | UGX |
| Kenya | KES |
| Tanzania | TZS |
| Nigeria | NGN |
| Ghana | GHS |
| South Africa | ZAR |
| United Kingdom | GBP |

More countries and currencies are added regularly. All transactions settle in ACoins regardless of the display currency.

## Security

<Note>
  AfuPay uses end-to-end encrypted transaction records and requires your AfuChat PIN or biometric authentication before every payment is confirmed. Never share your PIN with anyone. AfuChat support staff will never ask for your wallet PIN or one-time codes.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Enable **biometric authentication** in your AfuChat security settings for faster and safer payment confirmation without typing your PIN each time.
</Tip>
