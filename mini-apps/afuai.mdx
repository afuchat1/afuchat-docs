AfuAI brings a powerful AI assistant directly into your AfuChat experience — no external app, no account, and no extra setup required. Powered by **Engagera AI**, AfuAI appears in your inbox like a regular contact and is always ready to help you write, think, translate, summarise, and explore ideas. For visual questions, the companion **AfuLab** feature lets you point your camera at anything in the real world and get an instant AI-powered identification.

## Opening AfuAI

You can reach AfuAI from two places:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From the Apps tab">
    1. Tap the **Apps** tab in the bottom navigation bar.
    2. Tap the **AfuAI** card (look for the **AI** badge).
    3. The AfuAI chat opens instantly — start typing your question or request.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From your inbox">
    AfuAI appears as a pinned contact in your chat inbox. Tap the **AfuAI** conversation to open it directly, just like messaging a friend.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## AfuLab — visual AI identification

AfuLab (also listed as **AfuLab** in the Apps tab) is the visual intelligence feature of AfuAI. Open your camera, point it at something, and AfuLab identifies it in seconds using on-device and cloud AI.

### Opening AfuLab

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From AfuAI">
    While chatting with AfuAI, tap the **camera icon** in the input bar to switch to AfuLab's camera view.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From the Apps tab">
    Tap **Apps**, then tap **AfuLab** (the card with the camera icon and **AI** badge) to open the camera directly.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Understanding AfuLab results

After AfuLab analyses your image, it returns a structured result card:

| Field | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| **Title** | The name of the identified subject |
| **Description** | A concise explanation of what it is |
| **Facts** | Interesting or useful facts about the subject |
| **Category** | One of: food, plant, animal, place, product, person, artwork, text, object, or other |
| **Confidence** | How certain the AI is about the identification (shown as a percentage) |
| **Suggested search** | A ready-made search query to explore further |

<Tip>
  AfuLab works best in **good lighting**. Move closer to your subject, avoid motion blur, and make sure the object fills most of the camera frame for the highest confidence results.
</Tip>

<Note>
  AfuLab processes images using Engagera AI. Images are analysed to generate a response and are not stored permanently. Review AfuChat's Privacy Policy for full details on how AI features handle your data.
</Note>
