AfuMarket brings a full in-app shopping experience to AfuChat. Browse and purchase products from **verified stores and individual sellers**, pay instantly with ACoins, and track your orders — all without leaving the app. Whether you're a shopper looking for great deals or a seller ready to reach AfuChat's growing user base, AfuMarket is your marketplace.

## Opening AfuMarket

Tap the **Apps** tab in the bottom navigation bar, then tap the **AfuMarket** card (look for the **NEW** badge). AfuMarket opens to a curated home feed with featured products, trending stores, and recommended categories.

## Browsing and shopping

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse by category">
    Use the category bar at the top of AfuMarket to filter products — fashion, electronics, food, beauty, home goods, and more. Tap any category to see all available listings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a product page">
    Tap any product card to open its full listing. Each product page shows high-resolution images, a full description, price in ACoins (with local currency equivalent), seller profile, star ratings and reviews, and available shipping options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to cart">
    Select your preferred size, colour, or variant (if applicable), then tap **Add to Cart**. You can continue browsing and add multiple items before checking out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Checkout with ACoins">
    Tap the cart icon, review your items and totals, then tap **Checkout**. Confirm the delivery address and tap **Pay with ACoins** to complete your order instantly using your AfuPay balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You need sufficient ACoins in your AfuPay wallet to complete a purchase. If your balance is low, tap **Top Up** during checkout to add ACoins before confirming.
</Note>

## Tracking your orders

After placing an order, you can follow its progress at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My Orders">
    In AfuMarket, tap your **profile icon** or the menu and select **My Orders**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an order">
    Tap any order in the list to see its current status — Confirmed, Processing, Shipped, Out for Delivery, or Delivered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the tracking link">
    For shipped orders, tap **Track Shipment** to see real-time courier updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every product page surfaces the seller's details so you can shop with confidence:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Verified seller badge" icon="circle-check">
    Stores and sellers with a verification badge have been reviewed by the AfuMarket team. Look for the checkmark on the seller profile.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ratings and reviews" icon="star">
    See the seller's overall star rating and read individual buyer reviews directly on the product page before you buy.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shipping info" icon="truck">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Seller profile" icon="user">

## Opening your own store

Ready to sell? Any AfuChat user can apply to open a store on AfuMarket.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the store application">
    In AfuMarket, tap the menu and select **Open a Store**, or navigate to the seller onboarding section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your seller profile">
    Provide your store name, category, contact details, and a brief description of what you sell. Upload a store logo or banner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    Tap **Submit Application**. The AfuMarket team reviews your application to verify it meets the platform's seller standards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get approved and list products">
    Once approved, you can start adding product listings — photos, descriptions, pricing in ACoins, and stock quantities.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing your store

Once your store is live, access your seller dashboard at any time:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your store dashboard">
    In AfuMarket, tap your profile and select **Manage My Store**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add and edit listings">
    Create new product listings, update prices, edit descriptions, and mark items as in stock or sold out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review incoming orders">
    Check new orders, mark items as shipped, and add tracking numbers so buyers can follow their delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View store analytics">
    See views, sales, and earnings summaries to understand how your store is performing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Stores with clear photos, accurate descriptions, and fast response times earn higher ratings — making them more visible to shoppers in the AfuMarket home feed.
</Tip>
