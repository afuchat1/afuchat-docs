Beyond the flagship experiences of AfuPay, AfuAI, and AfuMarket, AfuChat packs a full suite of additional mini-apps — each designed to make something you already do easier, faster, or more fun without ever leaving the app. Open the **Apps tab**, tap any card, and you're in. Here's a guide to everything else available to you.

---

## Entertainment & social

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AfuGames" icon="gamepad">
    **Play games and compete with friends.**

    AfuGames is your in-app gaming hub. Launch casual and competitive games, challenge your AfuChat contacts to head-to-head matches, and climb the leaderboard. New games are added regularly — check back for the latest titles. Open from the Apps tab and tap any game to start playing instantly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AfuGifts" icon="gift">
    **Send animated gifts to people you love.**

    Celebrate birthdays, anniversaries, achievements, or any moment worth marking with a beautiful animated gift. Browse the gifts catalogue, pick one that fits the occasion, and send it to any AfuChat user. The recipient sees a full-screen animation right inside their chat. Gifts are purchased with ACoins.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AfuMatch" icon="heart">
    **Meet new people and find meaningful connections.**

    AfuMatch lets you discover and connect with new people on AfuChat based on shared interests and location. Set up a short profile, browse suggested profiles, and start a conversation when there's a mutual match. All interactions happen inside AfuChat — your main account privacy settings remain fully under your control.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Events" icon="calendar-days">
    **Discover local and online events near you.**

    The Events mini-app surfaces local meetups, concerts, community gatherings, and virtual events relevant to your location and interests. Tap an event to see the details, RSVP, and share it with friends via AfuChat messages or channels. Organisers can also create and publish events directly from the app.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Work & earning

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AfuFreelance" icon="briefcase">
    **Hire talent or find work on AfuFreelance.**

    AfuFreelance is the platform's built-in gig marketplace. If you have a skill — design, writing, coding, video editing, translation, and more — you can list your services and get hired by other AfuChat users. Clients post jobs, freelancers apply, and payments are made in ACoins directly through AfuPay. Look for the **NEW** badge in the Apps tab.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AfuBusiness" icon="chart-line">
    **Tools and analytics for your business.**

    AfuBusiness gives merchants, creators, and brands a dedicated dashboard inside AfuChat. Track your channel and store performance, manage customer interactions, run promotions, and access insights on your audience growth — all in one place.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Referral" icon="users">
    **Invite friends and earn Nexa rewards.**

    Every time a friend you invite signs up and becomes active on AfuChat, you earn **Nexa** reward points. Open the Referral mini-app to get your unique invite link or QR code, track how many friends have joined through your link, and see your total Nexa earnings. Nexa can be used to unlock premium features and perks across AfuChat.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Usernames" icon="at">
    **Buy and sell premium @handles.**

    Short, memorable usernames are valuable. The Usernames marketplace lets you browse available premium @handles, place a bid, or list a username you own for sale. Transactions are settled in ACoins. Owning a premium username makes your AfuChat profile easier to find and share.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Identity & utility

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Digital ID" icon="id-card">
    **Your verifiable digital identity card.**

    Digital ID generates a **3D-rendered identity card** tied to your AfuChat account. It displays your verified name, username, profile photo, and a scannable QR code that others can use to confirm your identity. Use it wherever you need to share a verifiable digital credential — events, marketplaces, or online communities.
  </Card>
  <Card title="QR Scanner" icon="qrcode">
    **Scan any QR code — links, Wi-Fi, contacts and more.**

    The QR Scanner mini-app gives you an instant, full-screen camera scanner that handles any standard QR code. Scan website links, Wi-Fi network credentials, contact cards, AfuPay payment QRs, event check-ins, and more. Results are displayed immediately with a one-tap action button.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AfuServices" icon="bolt">
    **Pay bills, top up airtime, and access local services.**

    AfuServices connects you to everyday essential services right inside AfuChat. Top up mobile airtime for yourself or someone else, pay utility bills, buy data bundles, and access other local service payments — all settled instantly with ACoins. Supported services vary by country and are updated regularly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AfuSearch" icon="magnifying-glass">
    **Search people, posts, channels, and more.**

    AfuSearch is a dedicated, full-scope search experience that goes beyond the basic search bar. Find people by name or username, discover public posts and channels, search for products on AfuMarket, and explore events — all from a single search interface.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Collections & saves

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Saved" icon="bookmark">
    **Saved posts, starred messages, and your collections.**

    Every post you bookmark and every message you star in AfuChat is stored here in one organised place. Filter by saved posts, starred chat messages, or browse your personal collections. Tap any saved item to jump straight back to it in context.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collections" icon="layer-group">
    **Curate and share themed collections.**

    Collections lets you build themed boards of content — links, posts, images, products, and more — and share them publicly or privately. Create a collection for a trip you're planning, products you love, or resources you want to share with your community. Others can follow your collections and you can follow theirs.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Mini-apps marked **NEW** have been recently launched and may be rolling out gradually. If you don't see an app in your Apps tab yet, make sure you're running the latest version of AfuChat.
</Note>

<Tip>
  You can long-press any mini-app card in the Apps tab to pin your favourites to the top of the launcher for faster access.
</Tip>
