ACoins are the currency that powers AfuChat. Whether you are tipping a creator, subscribing to a Premium plan, buying a digital badge, or unlocking a paid live stream, ACoins are how value moves on the platform. They are simple to understand, easy to top up, and accepted everywhere inside AfuChat.

## The basics

**1 ACoin = $0.01 USD.** That fixed rate makes it straightforward to understand what anything on AfuChat costs in real-world terms.

| ACoins | USD equivalent |
|---|---|
| 100 ACoins | $1.00 |
| 500 ACoins | $5.00 |
| 1,000 ACoins (1K) | $10.00 |
| 10,000 ACoins (10K) | $100.00 |
| 1,000,000 ACoins (1M) | $10,000.00 |

<Note>
  AfuChat displays large ACoin amounts in shorthand: values of 1,000 or more appear as **XK** (e.g. 2.5K), and values of 1,000,000 or more appear as **XM** (e.g. 1.2M). This shorthand is display-only — your full balance is always shown in your wallet.
</Note>

## ACoins vs. Nexa — what is the difference?

AfuChat has two types of in-app value: **ACoins** and **Nexa**. They are not interchangeable, so it is worth understanding what each one is.

| | ACoins | Nexa |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Purchasable in-app currency | Reward points |
| **How you get it** | Top up via AfuPay | Earned through referrals and platform activity |
| **Can you buy it?** | Yes | No — Nexa cannot be purchased |
| **What it's used for** | All transactions on AfuChat | Platform rewards and recognition |
| **Converts to ACoins?** | N/A | Subject to current reward terms |

<Tip>
  You earn **Nexa** every time you refer a new user who becomes active on AfuChat. Nexa points contribute to your overall platform standing and can unlock reward milestones. Check **Profile → Refer & Earn** to see your Nexa balance and referral link.
</Tip>

## How to top up ACoins

You add ACoins to your wallet through **AfuPay**, AfuChat's built-in payment system. AfuPay supports local currencies in over 20 countries, so you pay in the currency that is most convenient for you.

1. Open **AfuChat** and tap **Wallet** in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **Top Up**.
3. Select a top-up amount or enter a custom amount.
4. Choose your preferred payment method in AfuPay.
5. Confirm the payment. ACoins appear in your wallet instantly.

## Checking your balance

Your ACoins balance is always visible in your **Wallet**. You can also see it as a quick-glance figure at the top of the Premium and Creator Dashboard pages whenever a transaction is required.

AfuChat also shows your local currency equivalent alongside your ACoin balance so you always know the real-world value of what you are spending.

## Understanding spending errors

If a transaction fails, AfuChat shows one of two error messages:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Insufficient ACoin balance">
    **What it means:** Your wallet does not have enough ACoins to complete the transaction.

    **What to do:** Go to **Wallet → Top Up** and add more ACoins, then try the transaction again. There is no charge for a failed transaction — no ACoins are deducted when a payment does not go through.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Invalid amount">
    **What it means:** The amount entered is zero, negative, or otherwise not accepted by the system.

    **What to do:** Check the amount you entered and make sure it meets the minimum required for that transaction. Each creator or feature sets its own minimum — this is always displayed on the payment screen before you confirm.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Security and transaction history

Every ACoin transaction is recorded in your wallet's **Transaction History**. You can view a full log of every top-up, purchase, tip sent, tip received, and subscription charge from **Wallet → Transaction History**.

<Warning>
  AfuChat will **never** ask for your payment credentials outside of the AfuPay flow inside the app. If anyone contacts you claiming to top up your ACoins through an external link or asks for your payment details directly, do not share them and report the account immediately.
</Warning>
