AfuChat is built for creators who want to earn directly from the people they engage with — no third-party platforms, no middlemen, no complex integrations. Every monetization tool runs inside AfuChat and settles payments in **ACoins**, the platform's in-app currency. You enable only the tools you want, set your own prices, and get paid automatically whenever someone meets your terms.

This page covers all 15 creator monetization features, organised by category.

---

## Messaging monetization

Your inbox is one of your most valuable assets. These tools let you decide who can reach you and what it costs them.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Paid Chat Requests" icon="message-dollar">
    **Charge others to start a conversation with you.**

    Set a one-time ACoins fee that anyone who wants to open a new chat with you must pay before you see their message. Great for public figures, consultants, and anyone who receives a high volume of unsolicited messages. You receive the payment as soon as the conversation is initiated — you do not have to reply to keep the coins.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pay-Per-Message" icon="coins">
    **Charge a fee for every message sent in a conversation.**

    Once pay-per-message is active, each new message from the other person in a chat costs them a set number of ACoins. Ideal for ongoing advice, coaching, or support relationships where your time has a clear per-interaction value.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paid Voice Notes" icon="microphone">
    **Lock your voice messages behind a paywall.**

    When you send a voice note with this feature enabled, the recipient sees a locked preview and must pay your set fee to listen. Your voice content stays exclusive to those who value it enough to pay for it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paid Broadcasts" icon="bullhorn">
    **Send premium messages that followers pay to unlock.**

    Broadcast a message to all your followers, but keep the full content locked. Followers see a teaser and pay a flat ACoins fee to read or view the complete message. Perfect for announcements, exclusive updates, or time-sensitive content.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  You can combine messaging monetization tools. For example, you can require a paid chat request to start a conversation **and** charge per message once the chat is open — the fees stack and both go directly to your wallet.
</Tip>

## Profile monetization

Your profile is your storefront on AfuChat. These tools turn visits and your identity itself into revenue.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Profile View Fee" icon="eye">
    **Charge other users to view your full profile.**

    Set an ACoins price to unlock your profile. Visitors see a preview with your name and avatar, but your bio, posts, and media stay hidden until they pay. Useful for exclusive creators who want to gate their entire presence.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Username Marketplace" icon="at">
    **Buy and sell @handles on AfuChat.**

    List your current username for sale at any price you choose, or browse the marketplace to acquire a handle that fits your brand. Transactions are settled in ACoins instantly, and the username transfers to the buyer's account automatically on purchase.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Digital Badges" icon="certificate">
    **Create and sell custom badges.**

    Design your own badges and sell them to your followers. Buyers display your badge on their profile, signalling their support or membership. A powerful tool for fan communities, loyalty programs, and branded collectibles.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Content monetization

Your posts, streams, and media are your primary creative output. These tools let you charge for access to that content directly.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Paid Live Streams" icon="video">
    **Run paid live streams with an entry fee.**

    Set a ticket price in ACoins that viewers must pay before joining your live stream. You can set different price points for different streams — a casual Q&A might be lower than an exclusive masterclass or performance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paid Links" icon="link">
    **Put exclusive links behind a paywall.**

    Share a link — to a document, external resource, download, or any URL — and require an ACoins payment before the link is revealed. Useful for selling digital files, referral codes, discount links, or any exclusive resource.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Content Licensing" icon="file-contract">
    **Sell usage rights to your posts and media.**

    Offer other users or businesses a licence to use your photos, videos, or written content. Set your licensing fee in ACoins, define the usage terms, and receive payment directly. Your original content remains attributed to you.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Build paid communities, host events, and offer your skills as a service — all without leaving AfuChat.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Paid Communities" icon="users">
    **Create private groups with a membership fee.**

    Set up a community with a recurring or one-time ACoins membership fee. Only paying members gain access to the group's content, discussions, and exclusive posts. You control the price, the content, and who gets accepted.

    **Sell tickets to digital events.**

## How to enable creator tools

All monetization features are managed from a single dashboard. Your Premium plan tier determines which tools are available to you — higher tiers unlock more of the suite.

1. Open **AfuChat** and go to **Profile → Creator Dashboard**.
2. Browse the **Monetize** section to see all available tools.
3. Tap any tool to configure it — set your price, add a description if required, and toggle it on.
4. Your settings take effect immediately. AfuChat starts enforcing the paywall for new interactions right away.

<Accordion title="Changing or disabling a tool">
  You can update your prices or turn off any monetization tool at any time from the same Creator Dashboard. Changes apply to new interactions immediately. Ongoing sessions — like an active paid chat — continue under the terms that were active when they started.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="When do I receive payment?">
  ACoins are transferred to your wallet at the moment of each transaction. There is no holding period for most tools. For freelance services, payment is released from escrow once the client marks the work as complete, or automatically after the agreed delivery window passes without a dispute.
</Accordion>

## Payment flow

Every creator tool on AfuChat settles payments through the same underlying system. When a user pays to access your content or service, AfuChat deducts ACoins from their wallet and credits your wallet in the same transaction. You can see a full breakdown of every payment in your **Wallet → Transaction History**.

<Note>
  AfuChat applies a platform service fee to creator tool transactions. The fee is shown clearly on your Creator Dashboard and in your transaction history. The price you set is what you receive after the fee — there are no hidden deductions.
</Note>

For more detail on how ACoins work, see the [ACoins](/monetization/acoin) page.
