Prestige is AfuChat's spend-based ranking system that rewards your ongoing activity on the platform. Unlike your Premium subscription — which you pay for on a billing cycle — Prestige is calculated automatically from the **total ACoins you have spent** across everything AfuChat offers: tipping creators, buying in AfuMarket, unlocking services, and more. Your Prestige rank only ever goes up, and it never resets.

## How Prestige is calculated

Every time you spend ACoins anywhere on AfuChat, your cumulative lifetime spend increases. Your current Prestige tier is simply the highest threshold your total spend has crossed. There is nothing to claim or activate — the moment your spend reaches the next threshold, your rank upgrades and the new perks appear on your profile automatically.

<Note>
  Prestige is based on ACoins **spent**, not ACoins held. Topping up your wallet does not move you up a tier — you need to actually spend those coins on the platform.
</Note>

## How to view your Prestige progress

You can check your current tier and see how close you are to the next one at any time.

1. Open **AfuChat** and tap your profile avatar.
2. Scroll to the **Prestige** section of your profile page.
3. Your current tier badge and the ACoins needed to reach the next tier are displayed.

<Tip>
  Every purchase you make on AfuChat — tipping a creator, subscribing to a paid community, buying a badge — counts toward your Prestige progress. You do not need to do anything special; just use the platform the way you normally would.
</Tip>

## Status Goods

Status Goods are exclusive cosmetic items available in the **Prestige Shop**. They include custom avatar frames, message sticker packs, profile themes, and other collectibles that are only accessible at certain Prestige tiers.

<Accordion title="How Status Goods work">
  - You can browse the Prestige Shop from **Profile → Prestige → Shop**.
  - Items are categorised by the minimum tier required to purchase them (Silver, Gold, Diamond, Obsidian, or Legend).
  - Once purchased, Status Goods are yours permanently — they do not expire even if your spending level changes in the future.
  - Legend members have access to the **full catalogue**, including retired and limited-edition items.
  - Purchasing Status Goods counts toward your cumulative Prestige spend, which means shopping the Prestige Shop also helps you climb tiers faster.
</Accordion>

## The Rich List

The Rich List is AfuChat's public leaderboard of top spenders. Your placement on it is determined entirely by your lifetime ACoins spent.

- **Bronze** — you appear on the Rich List
- **Diamond** — you receive featured placement on the Rich List
- **Obsidian** — guaranteed Top 100 position while you hold the tier
- **Legend** — permanent Top 10 placement and a dedicated showcase on the Discover page

<Note>
  The Rich List is public. Other AfuChat users can browse it to discover top community contributors. Your username and Prestige tier are shown; no financial information is ever displayed.
</Note>

## Prestige vs. Premium

Prestige and Premium are two separate systems that coexist on your account. See the comparison table on the [Premium](/monetization/premium) page for a full breakdown, but the short version is:

- **Premium** is a subscription you pay for on a billing cycle — it unlocks features and higher limits.
- **Prestige** is a lifetime rank earned automatically through spending — it unlocks cosmetic perks and social status.

You can be a Free-tier Premium user with Legend Prestige, or a Platinum subscriber at Bronze Prestige. Both systems reward engagement in different ways.
