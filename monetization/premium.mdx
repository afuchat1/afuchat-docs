AfuChat Premium gives you access to enhanced features, higher usage limits, and exclusive badges that set your profile apart. Plans are tiered so you can start at whatever level fits your needs and upgrade any time. All Premium plans are purchased with **ACoins**, AfuChat's in-app currency.

## The four tiers

AfuChat has four membership tiers. The Free plan gives you full access to the core AfuChat experience. Upgrading to a paid tier layers on progressively more powerful creator tools, higher limits, and cosmetic perks.

<Note>
  Each tier **includes** everything in the tiers below it. Upgrading to Gold, for example, gives you all Silver perks plus Gold-exclusive features on top.

## How to subscribe

Subscribing to Premium takes just a few taps. Make sure you have enough ACoins in your wallet before you start — the full plan cost is charged at the point of subscription.

1. Open **AfuChat** and tap your profile avatar in the top-left corner.
2. Select **Premium** from your profile menu.
3. Browse the available plans and tap the tier you want.
4. Review the features and pricing, then tap **Subscribe**.
5. Confirm the ACoins payment. Your plan activates instantly.

<Tip>
  You can check your ACoins balance any time from your **Wallet** tab before heading to the Premium page.
</Tip>

## Earning Platinum through referrals

You do not have to pay for Platinum to enjoy its perks temporarily. AfuChat's referral program can grant you **Platinum status for a limited period** when you successfully refer new users to the platform.

When a referral milestone is reached, your account is upgraded to Platinum automatically and your subscription reflects a `platinum_until` date showing exactly when the complimentary period ends. If you want to keep Platinum access after that date, simply subscribe to the paid Platinum plan before it expires.

<Accordion title="How referral-based Platinum works">
  1. Share your unique referral link from **Profile → Refer & Earn**.
  2. When enough referred users join and become active, AfuChat credits you with a Platinum period.
  3. You receive a notification with the exact expiry date.
  4. All Platinum features are available for the full duration — there is no difference in access compared to a paid Platinum plan.
  5. You also earn **Nexa** reward points for each successful referral, which contribute to your overall platform standing.
</Accordion>

## Billing and auto-renewal

Premium plans **auto-renew** at the end of each billing cycle using ACoins from your wallet. AfuChat sends you a reminder notification before the renewal date so you are never caught off guard.

<Warning>
  Make sure your wallet has enough ACoins before your renewal date. If your balance is insufficient, your plan will downgrade to Free until you top up and resubscribe.
</Warning>

## How to cancel

You can cancel your Premium subscription at any time with no cancellation fee.

1. Go to **Profile → Premium → Manage Subscription**.
2. Tap **Cancel Plan**.
3. Confirm the cancellation.

Your Premium benefits remain active until the end of the current billing period. After that, your account returns to the Free tier. You will not be charged again unless you resubscribe.

## Premium vs. Prestige — what is the difference?

It is easy to confuse these two systems because they both reward engaged AfuChat users. Here is the key distinction:

| | Premium | Prestige |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | A paid subscription plan | A spend-based rank you earn automatically |
| **How you get it** | Purchase with ACoins | Accumulate ACoins spent on the platform |
| **Tiers** | Free, Silver, Gold, Platinum | Bronze → Silver → Gold → Diamond → Obsidian → Legend |
| **Resets?** | Renews on a billing cycle | Never resets — your rank only goes up |
| **Main benefit** | Unlocks features and higher limits | Visual status, cosmetic perks, Rich List placement |

You can hold a Free Premium plan and still reach high Prestige tiers — and vice versa. The two systems complement each other but are completely independent. See the [Prestige](/monetization/prestige) page for full details on the rank system.
