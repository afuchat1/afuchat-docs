AfuChat is a mobile-first super-app built for real conversations, content, and community. When you open it for the first time, a short onboarding flow guides you from signup to your first message in about five minutes. This page walks you through every step so you know exactly what to expect.

## How onboarding works

The first-time setup follows a straightforward path: download the app, create your account, choose your unique handle, personalise your profile, and then land in your inbox where AfuAI is already waiting to say hello.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download and open AfuChat">
    Install AfuChat from the Google Play Store and open it. Tap **Get Started** on the welcome screen to begin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up with your email address and a password, or tap **Continue with Google** to use your existing Google account. Both methods are equally fast and secure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your @handle">
    Pick a unique handle — this is your permanent identity on AfuChat (for example, `@alex`). Your handle appears in your profile link and cannot be changed later, so choose carefully.

    <Note>
      Handles are lowercase, contain only letters, numbers, and underscores, and must be unique across all of AfuChat.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your profile">
    Add a display name, upload a profile photo, and write a short bio. You can skip these steps and come back later, but completing your profile helps other users find and recognise you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your AfuAI welcome message">
    Once your profile is created, AfuAI automatically sends you a personalised welcome message in your inbox. Open it to get tailored suggestions, app tips, and guidance based on your interests. AfuAI stays in your inbox permanently as your on-demand assistant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the app">
    You're in. Browse your home feed, visit Discover to find people and content, or head straight to the Chats tab to send your first message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your onboarding checklist

AfuChat tracks a short checklist to help you get the most out of the app right away. Completing these three actions unlocks a better, more personalised experience from day one.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Add a profile photo" icon="camera">
    Tap your avatar on your profile page and upload a photo. Profiles with photos receive significantly more follow requests and messages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Follow 3 people" icon="user-plus">
    Visit Discover or search by @handle to find friends, creators, or topics you care about. Following people populates your home feed immediately.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Send your first message" icon="message">
    Open the Chats tab and tap the compose icon to start a conversation. You can also message AfuAI directly if you're not sure who to reach out to first.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Select your interests" icon="star">
    Go to **Me → Edit Profile → Interests** and pick the categories that match your passions. This personalises your Discover feed and content recommendations.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Sign-up options at a glance

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Email and password" icon="envelope">
    Create an AfuChat-native account using any email address. You'll verify your email before your profile goes live.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Google sign-in" icon="google">
    Tap **Continue with Google** to authenticate instantly. AfuChat imports your name and profile photo from Google, which you can change at any time.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  If you signed up with Google, you can still add a password later from **Settings → Account → Security** to enable email login as a fallback.
</Tip>

## What happens after onboarding

Once `onboarding_completed` is marked on your account, AfuChat's full feature set becomes available:

- **Home feed** — posts from people you follow, plus algorithmically recommended content
- **Discover** — trending topics, suggested users, and nearby communities
- **Chats** — direct messages, group chats, and AfuAI assistance
- **Me tab** — your profile, creator tools, business settings, and app preferences

<Note>
  Some features — including monetisation tools, business analytics, and certain creator capabilities — require a Premium subscription or a higher Prestige tier. You can explore these at any time from the **Me** tab.
</Note>
