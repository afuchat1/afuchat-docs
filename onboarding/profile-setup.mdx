Your AfuChat profile is the first thing people see when they discover you on the platform. A complete profile builds trust, improves your visibility in Discover, and makes it easier for friends and followers to connect with you. Set aside a few minutes to fill in each section — the effort pays off immediately.

## Display name vs. @handle

These two fields look similar but serve very different purposes.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Display name" icon="pen">
    Your display name is the human-readable name shown on your profile, posts, and in chat. It can be your real name, a nickname, or a brand name. You can change it at any time from **Me → Edit Profile**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="@handle" icon="at">
    Your handle is your permanent, unique ID on AfuChat — once set during signup it cannot be changed. It appears in your profile URL and in deep links like `afuchat://yourhandle`. Choose it carefully.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Your @handle is permanent. Double-check spelling and capitalisation before finishing signup, because there is no way to change it afterwards.
</Warning>

## Setting your profile photo and banner

Visual identity matters on AfuChat. Both your avatar and your banner image are editable at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Tap the **Me** tab at the bottom of the screen, then tap your avatar or the **Edit Profile** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a profile photo">
    Tap your current avatar (or the placeholder icon) to open the image picker. Choose a photo from your gallery or take a new one with your camera. AfuChat automatically crops it to a circle, so centre your face or logo in the frame.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a banner image">
    Tap the banner area at the top of your profile card to upload a wide image. Banners display at roughly 3:1 aspect ratio — a landscape photo or branded graphic works best.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a high-resolution photo for your avatar. AfuChat displays avatars at multiple sizes across the app, and a clear image makes your profile stand out in search results and the Discover feed.
</Tip>

## Writing your bio

Your bio appears directly under your display name and is one of the first things visitors read.

- Keep it concise and clear — aim for two to three sentences.
- Mention what you post about so new visitors know what to expect.
- You can include a call to action, such as "DM me for collabs" or "Follow for daily tips."
- Emoji are supported and can help your bio stand out visually.

## Adding your website URL

If you have a personal site, portfolio, business page, or link-in-bio service, add it in the **Website** field under **Edit Profile**. The link appears as a tappable URL on your public profile.

## Country and region

Setting your country and region tells AfuChat where you are located. This information is used to personalise your **Discover** feed with locally relevant content, trending topics, and suggested users from your area. It is never shared publicly as a precise location.

Go to **Me → Edit Profile → Country** and select your country from the list, then choose your region or state.

## Selecting interests

Interests are the categories that power AfuChat's content recommendations. The more accurately you select them, the better your home feed and Discover results will be.

<Accordion title="Available interest categories">
  AfuChat's interest categories include (but are not limited to):

  - **Tech** — software, gadgets, AI, gaming
  - **Music** — genres, artists, live events
  - **Sports** — football, basketball, fitness, esports
  - **Art & Design** — illustration, photography, architecture
  - **Business** — entrepreneurship, finance, marketing
  - **Lifestyle** — food, travel, fashion, wellness
  - **Education** — science, history, learning resources
  - **Entertainment** — movies, TV, comedy, celebrity

  Select as many as apply. You can update your interests at any time from **Me → Edit Profile → Interests**.
</Accordion>

## Privacy settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Public vs. private account">
    Go to **Me → Edit Profile → Privacy** and toggle **Private Account** on or off. A public account lets anyone follow you and see your posts. A private account requires you to approve each follow request before that person can see your content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Online status visibility">
    Toggle **Show Online Status** to control whether your contacts and followers can see when you are active in the app. Turning this off hides your "active now" or "last seen" indicator from everyone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Even with a public account, your direct messages are always private. Only people you choose to chat with can see your conversations.
</Note>

## Enabling tipping

Tipping lets your followers send you **ACoins** as a way to support your content. To turn it on:

1. Go to **Me → Edit Profile**.
2. Scroll to the **Monetisation** section.
3. Toggle **Enable Tipping** on.

Once enabled, a **Tip** button appears on your public profile. Followers tap it to choose an amount and send ACoins directly to your wallet.

<Note>
  Tipping is optional and can be switched off at any time. ACoins received through tips are credited to your AfuChat wallet and can be used across the app or converted according to current wallet terms.
</Note>

## Profile completeness

A fully completed profile improves your ranking in search results and your visibility in the Discover feed. Aim to fill in:

- ✅ Profile photo
- ✅ Banner image
- ✅ Display name
- ✅ Bio
- ✅ Country and region
- ✅ At least five interests
- ✅ Website URL (if applicable)
