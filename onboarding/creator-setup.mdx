AfuChat is built for creators. From tipping and channels to 15 dedicated creator tools and algorithm-powered distribution through AfuShorts, the platform gives you everything you need to build an audience, engage your community, and earn from your content. Follow this guide in order to set yourself up for success from the start.

## Creator setup overview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Before anything else, make sure your profile is fully filled in. Creators with complete profiles rank higher in search and Discover, convert more casual visitors into followers, and inspire more trust when asking for support.

    At a minimum, ensure you have:
    - A clear profile photo and banner image
    - A compelling bio that explains what you create
    - Your country and region set correctly
    - At least five relevant interests selected

    See the [Profile Setup guide](/onboarding/profile-setup) for detailed instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable tipping">
    Tipping is the fastest way to start receiving financial support from your followers. Go to **Me → Edit Profile → Monetisation** and toggle **Enable Tipping** on.

    Once enabled, a **Tip** button appears on your public profile. Followers can tap it to send you ACoins directly to your AfuChat wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Channel">
    Channels let you broadcast content directly to subscribers — think of it as a one-to-many feed where only you (and admins you appoint) can post. Subscribers receive notifications for every new post.

    To create a Channel:
    1. Go to the **Me** tab.
    2. Tap **Creator Studio → Channels**.
    3. Tap **New Channel** and give it a name, description, and cover image.
    4. Choose whether it is **free** or **paid** (paid channels require Premium — see the next step).
    5. Tap **Create** to publish.

    Share your channel link with your followers to start building your subscriber base.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upgrade to Premium">
    Many of AfuChat's creator monetisation features are available only to **Premium** subscribers at **Silver tier or above**. Premium unlocks:

    - Paid channel subscriptions
    - Advanced creator tools in Creator Studio
    - Priority algorithmic distribution for AfuShorts
    - Increased daily tipping caps
    - Revenue-sharing participation

    To upgrade, go to **Me → Premium** and choose your plan.

    <Note>
      Even without Premium, you can still enable tipping, create free channels, and access the first tier of creator tools. Premium accelerates what's already available to you.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable creator tools from Creator Studio">
    AfuChat provides **15 creator tools** across categories like content scheduling, analytics, monetisation, and audience engagement. Activate them one by one from **Me → Creator Studio** as you need them — you don't have to enable everything at once.

    <Accordion title="Categories of available creator tools">
      Creator tools are grouped into the following areas:

      - **Publishing** — post scheduler, bulk upload, and AfuShorts editor
      - **Analytics** — detailed post metrics, audience growth tracking, and engagement reports
      - **Monetisation** — paid channels, digital product sales, tipping management, and revenue dashboard
      - **Engagement** — polls, Q&A sessions, countdown timers, and interactive stickers for posts
      - **Distribution** — hashtag optimiser, cross-post tools, and community spotlight submissions

      Some tools within each category require an active Premium subscription. Tools that require Premium are marked with a **crown icon** in Creator Studio.
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grow your Prestige tier">
    Prestige is AfuChat's loyalty and influence system. Your Prestige tier directly affects how widely your content is distributed and how prominently your profile appears in Discover.

    Spend ACoins on the platform — through tips to other creators, in-app purchases, or boosting your own content — to accumulate Prestige points and advance through the tiers.

    <Tip>
      Engaging authentically with the community (commenting, tipping, and participating in events) is the most sustainable path to building Prestige. Higher tiers surface your content to exponentially larger audiences.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Growing your audience as a creator

Technical setup is only half the equation. Here are the most effective strategies for building a following on AfuChat.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Post to AfuShorts consistently" icon="film">
    AfuShorts is AfuChat's short-video feed with algorithmic distribution. Even accounts with zero followers can go viral here. Post consistently — at least three to five times per week — to maximise your algorithmic reach.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Use hashtags on every post" icon="hashtag">
    Add relevant hashtags to your posts and AfuShorts to appear in hashtag feeds and search results. Use a mix of broad tags (high volume) and niche tags (high intent) for the best reach.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Engage with your community" icon="heart">
    Reply to comments, tip creators in your niche, and participate in community discussions. Accounts that engage authentically receive higher Discover placement and stronger follower loyalty.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Promote your profile QR code" icon="qrcode">
    Share your AfuChat QR code in your other social profiles, on stream overlays, in email newsletters, or on physical materials to drive new followers from outside the platform.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Key tips for new creators

<Accordion title="How do I know which creator tools to enable first?">
  Start with the tools that align with your current goals:

  - **Just starting out?** Enable the post scheduler and AfuShorts editor so you can publish consistently without being online in real time.
  - **Building a community?** Turn on Q&A sessions and polls to drive engagement on your posts.
  - **Ready to monetise?** Enable the tipping management tool and, once you have Premium, set up a paid channel for your most dedicated followers.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between a Channel and a regular post?">
  Your regular posts appear in your followers' home feeds and on your profile. A Channel is a separate subscription-based feed where only you can broadcast — followers must actively subscribe to it. Channels are ideal for exclusive content, behind-the-scenes updates, or paid tiers of your community.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I monetise without Premium?">
  Yes. Tipping is available on all accounts regardless of Premium status. However, paid channel subscriptions, digital product sales, and several advanced monetisation tools require a Premium subscription at Silver tier or above. Start with tipping to generate your first earnings, then consider upgrading to unlock the full creator economy toolkit.
</Accordion>

## Next steps

Once your creator profile is live and your first tools are enabled, explore the full capabilities available to you:

- Review your analytics regularly from **Me → Creator Studio → Analytics** to understand what content resonates with your audience.
- Experiment with different post formats — text, images, AfuShorts, and polls each attract different types of engagement.
- Connect with other creators through AfuChat communities to collaborate, co-create, and cross-promote.

For a complete breakdown of all 15 creator tools and how to use each one effectively, visit the **Creator Tools** reference page.
