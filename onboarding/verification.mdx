Verification on AfuChat is a signal of authenticity. A verified badge tells the community that AfuChat has confirmed the identity behind an account, reducing impersonation and helping users trust the profiles they interact with. There are two distinct verification badges, each designed for a different type of account.

## The two verification badges

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Personal verified badge" icon="circle-check">
    A blue checkmark displayed next to your name on your profile, in search results, and alongside your posts. This badge confirms you are who you say you are as a public individual.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Organisation verified badge" icon="building-shield">
    A badge specific to organisations, institutions, and registered businesses. It signals that AfuChat has verified the legitimacy of the entity behind the account, beyond the identity of any single person.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who is eligible

Verification is not available to every account — AfuChat reviews applications based on notability, authenticity, and account activity.

<Accordion title="Personal verification eligibility">
  You may be eligible for a personal verified badge if you are:

  - A **public figure** — celebrities, athletes, journalists, politicians, academics, or social media creators with a substantial following
  - A **professional** in a field where identity verification matters — doctors, lawyers, licensed practitioners
  - A **well-known individual** referenced in mainstream media or credible public sources
  - An account at **risk of impersonation** due to public prominence

  Your account must be:
  - Complete (profile photo, bio, and active posting history)
  - Public (private accounts cannot be verified)
  - In good standing with AfuChat's Community Guidelines
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Organisation verification eligibility">
  You may be eligible for an organisation verified badge if your account represents:

  - A **registered business** with verifiable legal documentation
  - A **media organisation** (newspaper, broadcaster, digital publication)
  - A **non-profit or NGO** with official registration
  - A **government body or public institution**
  - A **brand or company** with an established public presence

  The account must be operated by someone authorised to represent the organisation.
</Accordion>

## How to apply

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap the **Me** tab, then tap the **Settings** icon (gear icon) in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Account settings">
    Tap **Account** in the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Request Verification">
    Scroll to the **Verification** section and tap **Request Verification**. The application form opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your verification type">
    Choose between **Personal Verification** and **Organisation Verification** based on the nature of your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit required documents">
    Upload the supporting documents for your chosen verification type (see below). Make sure images are clear, unobstructed, and fully legible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and wait for review">
    Tap **Submit Application**. AfuChat's verification team reviews your submission and responds within the timeframe below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Required documents

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Personal verification" icon="id-card">
    Submit a government-issued photo ID such as a passport, national identity card, or driver's licence. The name on the ID must match your AfuChat display name or @handle.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Organisation verification" icon="file-certificate">
    Submit official business registration documents, a certificate of incorporation, or equivalent legal entity paperwork issued by a recognised authority in your country.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Do not submit expired documents. AfuChat's team will reject applications with documents that are past their validity date. Re-apply with a current document once you have renewed it.
</Warning>

---

## Review timeline

AfuChat aims to complete verification reviews within **3 to 7 business days** of receiving a complete application. You'll receive a notification in your AfuChat inbox once a decision has been made.

<Note>
  If your application is declined, AfuChat will provide a reason. Common reasons include insufficient public presence, unclear document photos, or a mismatch between your ID and your account name. You may re-apply after addressing the issue.
</Note>

## Business verification (additional step)

Business accounts that have enabled Business Mode can go through an additional business verification process that is separate from the standard personal or organisation badge.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Business Mode">
    Ensure your account has Business Mode switched on. See the [Business Account guide](/onboarding/business-account) for instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the business verification screen">
    Navigate to `afuchat://business-verification` from within AfuChat, or go to **Settings → Account → Business Verification**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the business verification form">
    Provide your business name, registration number, registered address, and upload your business registration certificate or equivalent documentation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await approval">
    The business verification review follows the same 3–7 business day timeline. Once approved, your company pages and business profile display the organisation verified badge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Applying for business verification before launching any paid broadcast campaigns or sponsored content significantly increases trust signals for your audience and can improve campaign performance.
</Tip>

## After verification

Once your badge is approved:

- It appears immediately next to your name on your profile, in search results, and on all your posts.
- Verified accounts receive a slight boost in search ranking and Discover visibility.
- You cannot transfer a verified badge to a different account or @handle.
- Misuse of the platform after verification may result in the badge being revoked.
