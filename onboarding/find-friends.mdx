Building your network is one of the most rewarding parts of getting started on AfuChat. Whether you want to reconnect with people you already know or discover entirely new communities, AfuChat gives you four distinct ways to find and connect with others. This guide explains each method and clarifies the difference between following someone and adding them as a contact.

## Four ways to find people

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Import phone contacts" icon="address-book">
    AfuChat matches your saved phone numbers against registered accounts so you can find friends who are already on the platform.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search by @handle or name" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Use AfuSearch to find any public account by their unique handle or display name instantly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Scan a QR code" icon="qrcode">
    Every AfuChat profile has a unique QR code. Scan someone's code with your camera to open their profile directly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Browse Discover" icon="compass">
    The Discover feed surfaces people you may know, trending accounts, and community recommendations based on your interests and location.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### 1. Import phone contacts

AfuChat can cross-reference your phone's address book against its user database to surface contacts who have already signed up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Find Friends screen">
    Go to the **Discover** tab and tap **Find Friends**, or navigate to **Me → Contacts → Import Contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant the contacts permission">
    AfuChat will ask for permission to access your contacts (**READ_CONTACTS**). Tap **Allow** to proceed. This permission is used only for matching — your contacts are not stored on AfuChat's servers or shared with third parties.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review matched accounts">
    AfuChat displays a list of your contacts who are already on the platform. Tap **Follow** or **Add Contact** next to each person to connect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you deny the contacts permission, you can re-enable it later from your Android device's **Settings → Apps → AfuChat → Permissions → Contacts**.
</Note>

---

### 2. Search by @handle or display name

AfuSearch is AfuChat's built-in user and content search engine.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open search">
    Tap the **Search** icon in the top bar, or open the **Discover** tab and tap the search field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the handle or name">
    Enter the @handle (with or without the `@` symbol) or the display name of the person you are looking for. Results appear in real time as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the profile">
    Tap a result to open their full profile, then tap **Follow** or **Message** to connect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you know someone's exact @handle, you can also navigate directly to their profile using the deep link `afuchat://[handle]` — for example, `afuchat://alex`.
</Tip>

---

### 3. Scan a QR code

Every AfuChat profile includes a unique QR code that links directly to that profile. This is the fastest way to connect with someone in person.

**To find your own QR code:**
Go to **Me → Profile** and tap the **QR Code** icon near your avatar. Share it as an image or let someone scan your screen directly.

**To scan someone else's code:**
Open the **afuqr** mini-app from the Discover tab or the app launcher, point your camera at their QR code, and AfuChat opens their profile instantly. Tap **Follow** or **Message** to connect.

---

### 4. Browse Discover — People you may know

The **Discover** tab features a **People You May Know** section that updates regularly based on:

- Mutual connections and followers
- Shared interests you selected during profile setup
- Your country and region settings
- Recent activity and trending accounts in your area

Scroll through the suggestions and tap **Follow** on anyone that interests you. The more connections and interests you add, the better these recommendations become.

#### User discovery feed

You can also access a dedicated user discovery feed at `afuchat://user-discovery`. This surface highlights new and active users across AfuChat, making it easy to discover emerging creators and community members.

### Nearby friends

AfuChat can suggest users who are physically near you using your device's location data.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable location access">
    When prompted, allow AfuChat to access your location. You can set this to **While using the app** for privacy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse nearby users">
    Open Discover and scroll to the **Nearby** section to see suggested users in your vicinity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Your exact location is **never** shown to other users. AfuChat uses location data only to generate proximity-based suggestions — no coordinates, addresses, or precise location details are visible to anyone on the platform.
</Warning>

## Following vs. adding as a contact

These two connection types are different, and understanding the distinction helps you manage your network intentionally.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Following" icon="rss">
    When you follow someone, their public posts, AfuShorts, and updates appear in your home feed. Following is one-directional — they do not need to follow you back. This is the standard social connection on AfuChat.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Contact" icon="user-check">
    Adding someone as a contact enables direct messaging. Both people must mutually accept the contact request before a chat thread opens. Contacts also appear in your address book within AfuChat.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  On a **private account**, follow requests must be approved by the account owner before you can see their posts. Sending a message to someone who has not accepted your contact request places your message in their **Message Requests** folder.
</Note>
