AfuChat's Business Mode transforms your personal account into a professional presence with access to tools built specifically for brands, businesses, and organisations. Switching is free and takes less than a minute — and you can toggle it off at any time without losing any content or followers.

## What Business Mode unlocks

When you enable Business Mode, your account gains access to a dedicated set of features that are not available on standard personal accounts.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AfuBusiness dashboard" icon="chart-bar">
    A full analytics suite with post performance metrics, follower growth charts, audience demographics, and engagement breakdowns.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Company page creation" icon="building">
    Create one or more dedicated company pages with their own profiles, followers, and content feeds, separate from your personal account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Business verification badge" icon="badge-check">
    Apply for a business verification badge that displays on your profile and company pages, building trust with customers and partners.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Channel creation" icon="megaphone">
    Create broadcast channels to push announcements, updates, and offers directly to subscribers without the noise of a two-way feed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paid broadcast tools" icon="bullhorn">
    Access premium distribution tools to amplify your messages to targeted segments of AfuChat's user base.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Follower insights" icon="users">
    See detailed breakdowns of who follows your account — including location, interests, activity times, and engagement patterns.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to enable Business Mode

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap the **Me** tab at the bottom of the screen, then tap the **Settings** icon (gear icon) in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Account settings">
    Tap **Account** in the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Business Mode on">
    Find the **Business Mode** toggle and switch it on. AfuChat will confirm the switch and reload your account with business features enabled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Enabling Business Mode does not affect your existing posts, followers, or messages. All your existing content remains intact and your personal profile stays visible.
</Note>

## Creating a company page

A company page is a standalone profile for your business or organisation, distinct from your personal account. You can manage multiple company pages from a single AfuChat account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Me tab">
    Tap **Me** in the bottom navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Company section">
    Tap **Company** in the Me tab menu. If you haven't created a company page yet, you'll see a prompt to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Create">
    Tap **Create Company Page** and fill in your company details:
    - **Company name** — the public display name for your business
    - **Slug** — the unique URL identifier (for example, `afuchat.com/company/yourcompany`)
    - **Industry** — select the category that best describes your business
    - **Description** — a short summary of what your company does
    - **Logo and banner** — upload your brand assets
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your page">
    Tap **Publish** to make your company page live. It immediately becomes searchable and followable by other AfuChat users.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Choose your company slug carefully — it forms part of your public company page URL and is how users and search engines identify your brand on AfuChat. Like personal handles, slugs are unique and cannot be reused once claimed.
</Tip>

## Using the AfuBusiness mini-app

The AfuBusiness mini-app is your central command centre for all business activity on AfuChat. Access it from the **Me** tab after enabling Business Mode.

### Post analytics

View performance data for every post, including:
- **Impressions** — how many times your post appeared in feeds
- **Engagements** — likes, comments, shares, and saves
- **Reach** — unique accounts that saw the post
- **Click-through rate** — for posts containing links or calls to action

### Follower insights

The Followers section shows you who is following your account with breakdowns by:
- Geographic location (country and region)
- Interest categories
- Activity patterns (when your followers are most active)
- New vs. returning followers over time

### Audience breakdown

The Audience screen provides a demographic overview of your combined follower base, helping you tailor your content strategy to the people most engaged with your brand.

<Accordion title="How to access individual post analytics">
  1. Open any post from your business profile or company page.
  2. Tap the **…** menu in the top-right corner of the post.
  3. Select **View Insights**.
  4. Review the metrics panel that slides up from the bottom of the screen.
</Accordion>

## Switching back to a personal account

If you decide Business Mode isn't right for you, you can turn it off at any time.

Go to **Settings → Account** and toggle **Business Mode** off. Your account reverts to a standard personal profile, and business-only features (such as the AfuBusiness dashboard and company page management) are hidden until you re-enable it.

<Warning>
  Turning off Business Mode does not delete your company pages or analytics data. Your data is preserved and reappears if you re-enable Business Mode later.
</Warning>
