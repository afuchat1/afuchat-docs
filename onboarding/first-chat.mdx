Sending your first message on AfuChat takes only a few seconds. Whether you're reaching out to a friend, replying to a creator, or asking AfuAI for help, the chat experience is designed to be immediate and expressive. This guide covers every way to start a conversation and all the message types you can send.

## Three ways to start a conversation

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="From the Chats tab" icon="comments">
    Tap the compose icon in the top-right corner of the Chats tab, search for a contact or username, and tap their name to open a new thread.
  </Card>
  <Card title="From a profile" icon="user">
    Visit any user's profile and tap the **Message** button below their bio to open a direct chat thread with them instantly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Via deep link" icon="link">
    Open `afuchat://chat/new` from any browser or another app to jump straight into the new-chat composer inside AfuChat.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reply to AfuAI" icon="robot">
    AfuAI's welcome message is already in your inbox. Reply to it any time to ask questions, get recommendations, or explore the app with guided help.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Sending a message

Once a chat thread is open, you have a rich set of tools to express yourself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type your message">
    Tap the text field at the bottom of the screen and type your message. Press the **Send** button (arrow icon) to deliver it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add emoji">
    Tap the emoji icon on the left side of the text field to open the emoji picker. You can also type emoji directly using your keyboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach media">
    Tap the **+** icon to the left of the text field to open the attachment menu. From there you can:
    - Choose a **photo or video** from your gallery
    - **Take a photo** using your camera
    - Attach a **file** from your device storage
    - Share your **current location**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a voice note">
    Hold down the **microphone** button on the right side of the text field to record a voice note. Release to send. Slide left while holding to cancel the recording before it sends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  To send a longer voice note without holding the button the entire time, tap the microphone icon once to lock the recording, then tap **Send** when you're finished.
</Tip>

## Reacting to messages

You can react to any message with an emoji without sending a reply.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Long-press a message">
    Press and hold any message bubble until the reaction picker appears above it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a reaction">
    Tap any emoji from the quick-reaction bar. Your reaction appears underneath the message, visible to everyone in the thread.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a reaction">
    Long-press the message again and tap your existing reaction to remove it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Chatting with AfuAI

AfuAI is always available in your inbox as a dedicated chat thread. Unlike other conversations, you never need to wait for a reply — AfuAI responds immediately.

Use AfuAI to:
- Get help navigating any part of AfuChat
- Discover trending content or suggested accounts
- Brainstorm post ideas or captions
- Ask general questions or get personalised recommendations

<Note>
  The AfuAI chat thread is pinned at the top of your inbox and cannot be deleted. You can mute notifications for it from the thread settings if you prefer.
</Note>

## Starting a group chat

Group chats let you bring multiple people into the same conversation — great for friend groups, teams, or community planning.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the new group flow">
    Go to the **Chats** tab, tap the **+** icon, and select **New Group**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Search for and select the contacts you want to add to the group. You can add up to the group size limit, and you can always invite more people later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the group">
    Enter a group name in the text field. Choose something recognisable for all members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a group photo (optional)">
    Tap the camera icon next to the group name field to upload a photo that represents the group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the group">
    Tap **Create** to launch the group chat. All members receive a notification that they've been added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  As the group creator, you are automatically the admin. You can promote other members to admin, change the group name or photo, and manage member permissions from the group **Info** screen — tap the group name at the top of the chat to access it.
</Tip>

## Message status indicators

Keep track of whether your messages have been delivered and read.

<Accordion title="What the message tick icons mean">
  - **Single grey tick** — Message sent from your device to AfuChat's servers.
  - **Double grey tick** — Message delivered to the recipient's device.
  - **Double blue tick** — Message read by the recipient.

  Read receipts can be disabled by the recipient in their privacy settings. If you only see double grey ticks, it may mean the other person has turned off read receipts — not that they have not seen your message.
</Accordion>

<Warning>
  You can only message users who have accepted your contact request or who follow you, depending on their privacy settings. If your message does not appear in their main inbox, it lands in their **Message Requests** folder until they accept it.
</Warning>
