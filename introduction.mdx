## What makes AfuChat different

AfuChat is more than a messaging app. It is a **super-app platform** — a single destination where your social life, digital wallet, AI assistant, and marketplace all live side by side. Every feature is designed to work seamlessly on Android, with an offline-first architecture that keeps the app fast even on slow connections.

## Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download AfuChat">
    Install AfuChat on your Android device from the Google Play Store or via the direct APK link at [afuchat.com](https://afuchat.com).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up with your email or Google account. Choose a unique @handle that becomes your public identity on AfuChat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your profile">
    Add a profile photo, bio, and link your interests so AfuChat can personalise your Discover feed and match you with relevant communities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start exploring">
    Open the Chats tab to message contacts, tap Shorts to watch videos, and visit the Apps tab to explore AfuPay, AfuAI, and the full mini-app suite.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  AfuChat is currently available on Android (version 5.0+). iOS support is coming soon.
</Note>
