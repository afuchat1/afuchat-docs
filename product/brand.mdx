AfuChat's brand reflects who we are at our core — a warm, confident, and inclusive community platform built for the world's most connected generation. Every color choice, font selection, and word we write serves one goal: making every person who opens AfuChat feel like they truly belong.

## Brand Name & Tagline

The product is always written as **AfuChat** — one word, capital A and capital C. Never "Afu Chat", "afuchat", or "AFC". The official tagline is **"The Social Super-App"**, used in marketing materials, app store listings, and onboarding flows.

<Note>
  When referring to the app in writing, always use the full name **AfuChat** on first mention. Subsequent references on the same page may use "the app" for readability.
</Note>

### Color Usage Guidelines

- **Primary Blue (#085ed8)** should be the dominant color in any layout. Use it for primary buttons (which carry a 4 px corner radius), navigation indicators, and branded headings.
- **Accent Teal (#04c1ce)** supports the primary color and should never overpower it. Use it in gradient pairs, icon fills, and decorative backgrounds.
- **White (#ffffff)** is the preferred page and card background, keeping the interface clean and readable.
- **Dark neutral (#1a1a2e)** serves as the primary text color on light surfaces, ensuring strong contrast and legibility.

<Warning>
  Do not place primary blue text on the accent teal background, or vice versa. The contrast ratio between these two colors does not meet accessibility standards for body text.
</Warning>

## Typography

AfuChat uses **Times New Roman** for both headings and body text across all branded materials. This deliberate choice sets AfuChat apart from the sans-serif-heavy social media landscape — it lends the brand a sense of substance, storytelling, and editorial confidence.

| Element        | Typeface         | Style           |
|---------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Display Heading | Times New Roman | Bold, large     |
| Section Heading | Times New Roman | Semi-bold       |
| Body Copy       | Times New Roman | Regular         |
| Captions        | Times New Roman | Italic, small   |

<Tip>
  When Times New Roman is unavailable in a digital context (such as a web embed), fall back to **Georgia**, then **serif**. Never substitute a sans-serif font for branded AfuChat materials.
</Tip>

## Logo Usage

The AfuChat logo combines the wordmark with an optional icon mark. Follow these guidelines to keep the brand consistent everywhere it appears.

- Always maintain clear space of at least the height of the letter "A" in "AfuChat" around all sides of the logo.
- Use the full-color logo on white or very light backgrounds.
- Use the all-white logo on the primary blue or dark backgrounds.
- Do not stretch, skew, recolor, or add drop shadows to the logo.
- Do not place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without a solid color backing pad.

<Note>
  Official logo files are available to verified AfuChat partners and press outlets. Contact the AfuChat Technologies Ltd. team through the official website for brand asset requests.
</Note>

## Visual Personality

AfuChat's interface is built around a **liquid-glass design language** — translucent cards, vibrant blue-to-teal gradients, and soft-edged UI elements that feel tactile and alive. This visual style communicates:

- **Openness** — glass-like surfaces suggest transparency and honesty.
- **Energy** — gradient motion evokes progress, dynamism, and possibility.
- **Warmth** — rounded cards and generous white space make the experience feel welcoming rather than clinical.

Buttons across the app carry a **4 px border radius** — enough to soften harsh corners without sliding into the over-rounded pill shapes that can feel juvenile. This small detail reinforces the brand's balance between professionalism and approachability.
