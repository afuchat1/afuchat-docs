AfuChat was built on a simple but powerful belief: that Africa and its global diaspora deserve a social platform designed specifically for them — one that understands their culture, speaks their language, and grows their economic opportunities at the same time. That belief is what drives every product decision AfuChat Technologies Ltd. makes.

## Who We Are

**AfuChat Technologies Ltd.** is the company behind AfuChat — The Social Super-App. We are a technology company focused on mobile-first social infrastructure for Africa and the worldwide communities connected to it. Our team is distributed, our codebase is open, and our commitment to the people who use AfuChat is absolute.

## Our Vision

AfuChat's long-term vision is to become the **go-to super-app for Africa and the global African diaspora** — a single platform where people connect, communicate, discover content, access financial tools, shop, and participate in digital economies without needing to switch between a dozen different apps.

We believe that the next generation of transformative technology companies will be built in Africa, by African founders, for global audiences. AfuChat is our contribution to that future.

## The Super-App Model

A super-app is a single mobile application that bundles multiple distinct services into one seamless experience. Instead of downloading separate apps for messaging, payments, shopping, content, and AI assistance, you access all of them from one place — with one account, one identity, and one trusted platform.

AfuChat's super-app stack includes:

| Service      | What It Does                                                    |
|-------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **AfuChat** (core) | Social feed, short-form video (Shorts), messaging, communities |
| **AfuAI**   | AI-powered assistant and content tools built into the app        |
| **AfuPay**  | In-app payments and ACoin digital currency                       |
| **AfuMarket** | Social commerce — buy and sell within your community          |
| **Mini-Apps** | Third-party and first-party apps accessible inside AfuChat    |

<Tip>
  You don't need to sign up separately for any AfuChat service. Your AfuChat account — your handle, your ACoins balance, your reputation — works across every part of the super-app automatically.
</Tip>

## How AfuChat Evolves

AfuChat is not a finished product — it is a living platform that grows with its community. The roadmap is shaped by three forces:

1. **Community feedback** — Feature requests, bug reports, and conversations in AfuChat's own community channels directly influence what gets built next.
2. **Cultural relevance** — AfuChat monitors cultural moments, language trends, and creator behaviours across the African continent and diaspora to ensure the platform stays meaningful.
3. **Technology leadership** — From AI-assisted content creation to real-time translation and in-app commerce, AfuChat invests in capabilities that keep the platform ahead of user needs.

<Info>
  Because AfuChat is MIT-licensed and open-source, developers and community members can propose features through the public repository. Accepted contributions become part of AfuChat for everyone.
</Info>

## AfuChat and the Global African Diaspora

Tens of millions of people of African heritage live outside the continent — in Europe, North America, the Caribbean, South America, and beyond. They maintain deep ties to their home countries, their languages, and their cultures, but existing social platforms rarely reflect those identities.

AfuChat is designed to serve both sides of that connection simultaneously. Whether you are in Lagos or London, Accra or Atlanta, Nairobi or New York — AfuChat is your platform.
