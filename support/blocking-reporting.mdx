Your safety and comfort on AfuChat matter. Whether you're dealing with harassment, spam, or content that violates community standards, AfuChat gives you clear, easy-to-use tools to protect yourself and to flag harmful behavior for review. You don't need to tolerate unwanted interactions — and taking action is straightforward.

## Blocking a User

Blocking someone immediately cuts off all interaction between you and that person. It is one of the most powerful tools available to you, and the other person is **never notified** that they have been blocked.

### Block from a Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the user's profile">
    Tap on the user's name or profile photo anywhere in the app to open their profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the options menu">
    Tap the **⋯** (three-dot) menu in the top-right corner of their profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Block">
    Tap **Block** and confirm when prompted. The block takes effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Manage All Blocked Users

You can view and manage everyone you've blocked from a single screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Tap your profile photo, then tap **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Privacy → Blocked Users">
    Navigate to **Settings → Privacy → Blocked Users** (Settings → Blocked).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your blocked list">
    You'll see a list of every account you have blocked. From here you can unblock any of them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What Happens When You Block Someone

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Messages" icon="message-slash">
    The blocked user cannot send you new direct messages. Existing conversations are hidden from both sides.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stories & Content" icon="eye-slash">
    They cannot view your stories, posts, or profile while you have them blocked.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search" icon="magnifying-glass-minus">
    Your profile no longer appears in their search results, and theirs may be hidden from yours.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No Notification" icon="bell-slash">
    The blocked user is never told that they have been blocked. They simply can't interact with you.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Blocking is mutual in many respects — just as they cannot see your profile, your view of their profile will also be limited. You can unblock at any time.
</Note>

## Unblocking a User

If you change your mind, unblocking is just as simple as blocking.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Blocked Users">
    Navigate to **Settings → Privacy → Blocked Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the user">
    Scroll through your blocked list to find the person you want to unblock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Unblock">
    Tap the **Unblock** button next to their name and confirm. They will be able to interact with you again according to your regular privacy settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Unblocking does not automatically restore a previous follow relationship. If they were following you (or you were following them) before the block, those connections will need to be re-established.
</Note>

## Reporting a User or Content

Reporting sends a signal to the AfuChat safety team so they can review the account or content and take action according to the Community Guidelines. You can report both profiles and individual messages.

### Report a Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the user's profile">
    Navigate to the profile of the user you want to report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the options menu">
    Tap the **⋯** menu in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Report">
    Select **Report** from the menu. This opens the report flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a report category">
    Select the option that best describes the issue (see categories below) and add any additional context if prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Tap **Submit Report**. You'll receive a confirmation that your report has been received.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Report a Message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Navigate to the direct message or group chat containing the message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Long-press the message">
    Press and hold the specific message you want to report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Report">
    Tap **Report** from the action menu that appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category and submit">
    Pick the most relevant report reason and submit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Report Categories

<Accordion title="Spam">
  The account is sending unsolicited bulk messages, posting repetitive promotional content, or engaging in other spam-like behavior.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Harassment">
  The account is targeting you or others with threatening, abusive, or persistently unwanted contact.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Fake Account">
  The account is impersonating a real person, public figure, or organization, or appears to be operating under a false identity.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Inappropriate Content">
  The content violates AfuChat's Community Guidelines — for example, graphic violence, nudity in non-permitted contexts, or hate speech.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Other">
  Your concern doesn't fit neatly into the above categories. Please provide as much detail as possible in the text field so the safety team can understand the issue.
</Accordion>

<Note>
  All reports are confidential. The reported user is **never told** who submitted the report or the specifics of what was reported. Reports are reviewed by the AfuChat safety team, who may take actions including warnings, content removal, or account suspension depending on the severity of the violation.
</Note>

## Restricted List — A Softer Option

Not every situation calls for a full block. The **Restricted List** lets you quietly limit someone's ability to interact with your content without fully blocking them.

<Accordion title="What the Restricted List does">
  When you add a user to your restricted list:
  - Their **comments on your posts are hidden** from everyone else (only they can see their own comment)
  - You **won't receive notifications** for their likes, comments, or reactions
  - They **can still see your public profile** and send you messages, but those messages go to a separate filtered inbox
  - They are **not notified** that they've been restricted

  This is ideal for situations where a full block might be socially awkward — for example, a coworker, distant family member, or acquaintance whose interactions you'd prefer to tune out quietly.

  **To add someone to your Restricted List:** Go to their profile → **⋯** menu → **Restrict**, or manage the list directly at **Settings → Privacy → Restricted**.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  If you're unsure whether to block or restrict, start with the Restricted List. You can always escalate to a full block later if the behavior continues.
</Tip>
