## Submitting a Support Ticket

The in-app ticket system connects you directly with a support specialist and keeps a full history of your conversation in one place.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Help & Support">
    Tap the **Me** tab in the bottom navigation bar, then select **Help & Support**. Alternatively, open **afuchat://support** from any browser or link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'New Ticket' or 'Contact Us'">
    Select the option to create a new support request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category">
    Pick the category that best matches your issue — this routes your ticket to the right team and speeds up your response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your issue clearly">
    Include as much relevant detail as possible: what happened, what you expected to happen, and any steps you already tried. For billing issues, include your transaction reference or ACoins purchase receipt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and track">
    Submit the ticket. You'll receive an in-app notification when a support agent responds. View and reply to your tickets at any time under **Help & Support → My Tickets**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  After you submit a ticket, AfuChat's AI assistant may provide an immediate automated response with relevant help articles and quick fixes. If the automated response doesn't resolve your issue, a human support agent will follow up.
</Note>

## Response Times

<Accordion title="Standard Support Tickets">
  Most standard tickets — covering account questions, feature help, settings guidance, and general feedback — receive a response within **24 to 48 hours**, depending on current volume.

  To get a faster response:
  - Be specific about the issue in your initial message
  - Include your device model and Android version
  - Attach screenshots if the problem is visual
  - Avoid submitting duplicate tickets for the same issue (this can slow down the queue)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Safety & Security Issues (Priority Queue)">
  Reports involving account security, harassment, impersonation, or other safety concerns are routed to a **priority queue** and handled faster than standard tickets.

  If you believe your account has been compromised:
  1. Change your password immediately from **Settings → Manage Account**
  2. Review and revoke unfamiliar sessions at **Settings → Security → Active Sessions**
  3. Enable two-factor authentication at **Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication**
  4. Submit a ticket under the **Account Security** category

  <Warning>
    If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a real-world emergency, please contact your local emergency services. AfuChat support cannot provide emergency assistance.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Billing & ACoins Issues">
  For questions about ACoins purchases, AfuPay transactions, subscription charges, or refund requests, please include the following in your ticket to help us locate your case quickly:

  - **Transaction ID or reference number** (visible in your purchase receipt or AfuPay transaction history)
  - **Date and approximate time of the transaction**
  - **Amount charged and payment method used**
  - A description of the issue (e.g., ACoins not received, duplicate charge, payment declined)

  Billing tickets are typically addressed within **24 to 48 hours**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Bug Reports">
  Bug reports are invaluable to our engineering team. The more detail you provide, the faster we can identify and fix the issue. A great bug report includes:

  - Steps to reproduce the bug (what exactly did you tap or do?)
  - What you expected to happen vs. what actually happened
  - Screenshots or a screen recording showing the issue
  - Your device model and Android OS version
  - AfuChat app version (currently **2.2.8** — visible in Settings → About)

  Submit bug reports via **Me → Help & Support → Report a Bug**. The app may offer to attach crash logs automatically — please allow this if prompted, as it significantly helps the team.
</Accordion>
