Your AfuChat account settings give you full control over who you are on the platform, how the app behaves, and what happens to your data. Whether you want to update your display name, switch between accounts, or adjust how much storage the app uses, everything lives in one convenient place. Tap your profile photo, then select **Settings** to get started.

<Accordion title="Manage Account — Name, Handle, Email & Password">
  The **Manage Account** screen is your hub for all personal details tied to your AfuChat profile. Navigate there via **Settings → Manage Account**.

  **What you can update:**
  - **Display name** — the name other users see on your profile and in conversations
  - **@handle** — your unique username used for mentions and profile links (choose carefully; frequent changes may be limited)
  - **Email address** — used for account recovery and security notifications
  - **Password** — change your password at any time; we recommend using a strong, unique password
  - **Phone number** — link or update your phone number for added account recovery options

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Settings">
      Tap your profile photo in the bottom navigation bar, then tap **Settings**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Tap Manage Account">
      Select **Manage Account** from the settings list.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Edit a field">
      Tap any field you want to update, enter your new information, and confirm the change. Some updates (like email) may require you to verify the new address before the change takes effect.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Note>
    Changing your **@handle** updates your profile link across the app. Let people you chat with know about the change so they can still find you in search.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Linked Accounts — Add, Switch & Remove Accounts">
  AfuChat supports multiple accounts on a single device, so you can keep your personal and professional presences completely separate without logging in and out every time.

  **How linked accounts work:**
  - You can add several accounts and switch between them instantly
  - Each account has its own notifications, privacy settings, and content feed
  - Removing a linked account from the device does not delete the account — it simply signs you out on this device

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Settings">
      Go to **Settings** from your profile tab.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Tap your profile name at the top">
      At the top of Settings, tap your display name or the account switcher icon to see all linked accounts.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Add a new account">
      Tap **Add Account** and sign in or create a new AfuChat account.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Switch accounts">
      Tap any listed account to switch to it instantly. The app reloads with that account's feed and inbox.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Remove an account">
      Long-press an account in the switcher (or tap the **⋯** next to it) and choose **Remove from this device**.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Tip>
    Switching accounts is near-instant. If you run a creator page and a personal account, this feature lets you manage both without any hassle.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Notifications — Granular Push Notification Controls">
  AfuChat gives you fine-grained control over which events send you a push notification. You'll never be overwhelmed by pings you don't care about.

  Navigate to **Settings → Notifications** to see all available notification categories.

  **Types of notifications you can control:**
  - **Messages** — new direct messages and group chat activity
  - **Mentions & tags** — when someone mentions your @handle in a post or comment
  - **Reactions** — likes and emoji reactions on your content
  - **Follows** — new followers and follow requests
  - **Live alerts** — when accounts you follow go live
  - **System & account** — security alerts, account updates, and app announcements

  Each category has its own toggle, so you can enable only the notifications that matter most to you.

  <Note>
    If you're not receiving notifications at all, check that **push notifications are enabled** for AfuChat in your Android device settings under **Settings → Apps → AfuChat → Notifications**.
  </Note>

  <Tip>
    Enable **security alerts** even if you mute other notifications — they're the first line of defense if someone tries to access your account.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Storage Management — Clear Cache & Offline Videos">
  Over time, AfuChat caches images and videos locally to make your feed load faster. The **Storage** screen lets you see exactly how much space is being used and reclaim it whenever you need to.

  Go to **Settings → Storage** to view and manage your storage.

  **What you'll find:**
  - **Per-bucket storage stats** — see how much space is used by media cache, offline videos, and other content types individually
  - **Clear cached media** — removes temporarily stored images and videos; they'll re-download when you view that content again
  - **Offline videos** — AfuChat lets you save videos to watch without an internet connection; manage those downloads here

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Settings → Storage">
      Go to **Settings**, scroll down, and tap **Storage**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Review your usage">
      Each storage category shows how much space it is currently using on your device.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Clear what you don't need">
      Tap **Clear Cache** to free up space from temporary media files, or tap into **Offline Videos** to delete individual saved videos.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Note>
    Clearing the media cache does not delete any of your posts, messages, or account data — it only removes locally stored copies of media that can be re-downloaded at any time.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Language — Change the App Language">
  AfuChat supports multiple languages so you can use the app in whichever language feels most natural to you.

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Language Settings">
      Go to **Settings → Language** (you can also navigate directly via **Me → Language Settings**).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Choose your language">
      Browse or search the list of available languages and tap your preferred option.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Restart if prompted">
      Most language changes apply immediately. If prompted, restart the app to ensure all text updates correctly.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Tip>
    The app language setting affects the AfuChat interface only — it does not translate content posted by other users.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Danger Zone — Delete Your Account">
  Deleting your account is permanent and irreversible after the grace period. AfuChat gives you a **30-day window** after requesting deletion to change your mind and recover your account.

  <Warning>
    After the 30-day grace period, your account, profile, posts, messages, ACoins balance, and all associated data are **permanently and irreversibly deleted**. This action cannot be undone.
  </Warning>

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Back up your data first (recommended)">
      Before deleting, consider downloading your data via **Settings → Privacy → Data → Download Your Data** so you have a personal copy of your content and activity.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Open Settings → Danger Zone">
      Scroll to the bottom of Settings and tap **Danger Zone**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Request account deletion">
      Tap **Delete Account** and follow the on-screen confirmation steps. You may be asked to re-enter your password to confirm.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Grace period begins">
      Your account is immediately deactivated and hidden from other users. A 30-day countdown begins. Signing back in during this period will cancel the deletion.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Permanent deletion">
      If you don't sign back in within 30 days, your account is permanently deleted.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Note>
    If you simply want a break without losing your account, consider setting your account to **Private** (Settings → Privacy → Account) instead of deleting it.
  </Note>
</Accordion>
