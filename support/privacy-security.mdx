AfuChat is built around the idea that you should be in complete control of your experience — who can reach you, what others can see, and how your account is protected. This guide walks you through every privacy and security tool available to you. You can reach all of these settings via **Settings → Privacy** and **Settings → Security**, or by opening the deep links **afuchat://settings/privacy** and **afuchat://settings/security** directly.

## Privacy Settings

Your privacy settings let you shape exactly how you appear to the world and who is allowed to interact with you.

<Accordion title="Account Privacy — Public or Private">
  By default, AfuChat accounts are **public**, meaning anyone can view your profile and posts. Switching to a **private account** means only people you approve as followers can see your content.

  **Navigate to:** Settings → Privacy → Account

  | Setting | What it does |
  |---|---|
  | **Public** | Anyone on AfuChat can see your posts, stories, and profile |
  | **Private** | Only approved followers see your content; new follow requests require your approval |

  <Note>
    Switching to private does not remove existing followers — it only affects new requests going forward. Review your current followers list if you want a fresh start.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Visibility — Who Can See Your Profile">
  The **Visibility** settings give you more precise control beyond the public/private toggle.

  **Navigate to:** Settings → Privacy → Visibility

  Options typically include:
  - **Show online status** — toggle whether other users can see when you are active on AfuChat
  - **Profile visibility** — control which audiences (everyone, followers, no one) can view specific profile elements
  - **Story visibility** — choose who sees your stories separately from your main posts

  <Tip>
    Turning off **Show Online Status** is a great way to browse AfuChat without feeling pressured to respond immediately to messages.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Interactions — Who Can Mention or Tag You">
  **Navigate to:** Settings → Privacy → Interactions

  Control who is allowed to mention your @handle in posts and tag you in content:
  - **Everyone** — any AfuChat user can mention or tag you
  - **People you follow** — only accounts you follow can tag or mention you
  - **No one** — disables mentions and tags entirely

  This setting is especially useful for high-profile accounts or anyone who wants to limit unsolicited attention.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Messages — Who Can Send You Direct Messages">
  **Navigate to:** Settings → Privacy → Messages

  You can restrict who is allowed to start a new conversation with you:
  - **Everyone** — any user can message you
  - **People you follow** — only mutual or followed accounts can send you a DM
  - **No one** — closes your inbox to new conversations (existing threads are unaffected)

  <Note>
    Restricting messages to "People you follow" is a strong defense against unsolicited messages and spam without requiring you to block individuals.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Restricted List — Limit Interactions Without Blocking">
  The **Restricted List** is a softer alternative to blocking. When you add someone to your restricted list, they can still see your public profile and send you messages — but their interactions with your content are limited and less visible to others.

  **Navigate to:** Settings → Privacy → Restricted

  - Restricted users' comments on your posts are only visible to them (not your other followers)
  - You won't receive notifications for their activity
  - They are not informed that they have been restricted
  - You can remove anyone from the restricted list at any time

  <Tip>
    Use the Restricted List when you want to quietly reduce someone's presence in your experience without the finality of blocking — for example, with acquaintances or colleagues.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Data — Download a Copy of Your Information">
  AfuChat lets you request a full export of your personal data at any time.

  **Navigate to:** Settings → Privacy → Data

  **What you can download:**
  - **Profile** — your name, handle, bio, and profile photo
  - **Posts** — all content you've published
  - **Messages** — your conversation history
  - **Activity** — your interactions, likes, and comments
  - **Transactions** — your ACoins and AfuPay transaction history

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Privacy → Data">
      Go to **Settings → Privacy → Data**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Select data types">
      Check the boxes next to the types of data you want to include in your export.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Request the export">
      Tap **Download Your Data**. AfuChat will prepare your export — you'll receive a notification when it's ready.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Download the file">
      Open the notification or return to the Data screen to download your export file to your device.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Note>
    Data exports may take a few minutes to prepare depending on your account's history. Large accounts with extensive message history may take longer.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

## Security Settings

**Navigate to:** Settings → Security, or tap **afuchat://settings/security**

<Accordion title="Biometric App Lock — Fingerprint & Face ID">
  Biometric App Lock adds an extra layer of protection so that even if someone has physical access to your unlocked phone, they cannot open AfuChat without your fingerprint or face scan.

  **Navigate to:** Settings → Security → App Lock

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Enable App Lock">
      Toggle **Biometric App Lock** on. AfuChat will immediately prompt you to authenticate with your fingerprint or Face ID to confirm the change.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Choose lock timing">
      Select how quickly the lock engages after you leave the app — immediately, after 1 minute, after 5 minutes, etc.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Fallback PIN">
      If biometrics fail (e.g., wet hands), you'll be prompted for your AfuChat password as a fallback.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Note>
    Biometric App Lock uses your device's secure hardware (fingerprint sensor or face recognition). AfuChat never stores your biometric data.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)">
  Two-factor authentication protects your account even if your password is ever compromised. When enabled, signing in requires both your password and a one-time code.

  **Navigate to:** Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Two-Factor Authentication settings">
      Go to **Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Choose your 2FA method">
      AfuChat supports authentication apps (recommended) and SMS-based codes.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Scan the QR code or enter the setup key">
      If using an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Authy), scan the QR code displayed on screen.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Verify and save backup codes">
      Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator to confirm setup. **Save your backup codes** in a secure location — these let you regain access if you lose your authenticator device.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Warning>
    Store your 2FA backup codes somewhere safe and separate from your phone. If you lose access to your authenticator and your backup codes, account recovery becomes significantly harder.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Active Device Sessions — View & Revoke Access">
  AfuChat shows you every device and session currently signed in to your account. If you see anything unfamiliar, you can revoke that session immediately.

  **Navigate to:** Settings → Security → Active Sessions

  - Each session shows the device type, approximate location, and last active time
  - Tap **Revoke** next to any session to sign it out remotely
  - Tap **Sign Out All Other Devices** to clear every session except your current one

  <Warning>
    If you see a session you don't recognize, revoke it immediately and change your password. Consider enabling 2FA if you haven't already.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="OAuth Providers — Linked Social Accounts">
  AfuChat supports signing in via third-party providers such as Google. The **OAuth Providers** screen shows which external accounts are linked and allows you to unlink them.

  **Navigate to:** Settings → Security → OAuth Providers (or Settings → Linked Accounts)

  - Linking a provider lets you sign in without a password using that service
  - You can link multiple providers to the same AfuChat account
  - Unlinking a provider does not affect your AfuChat account — you can still sign in with your email and password

  <Note>
    If your only sign-in method is a linked social account, set an AfuChat password before unlinking it so you don't lose access.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

## Security Best Practices

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Use a Strong Password" icon="lock">
    Choose a password that is at least 12 characters and unique to AfuChat. Avoid reusing passwords from other services.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enable 2FA Today" icon="shield-check">
    Two-factor authentication is the single most effective step you can take to protect your account from unauthorized access.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Review Sessions Regularly" icon="display">
    Check your active sessions every few weeks and revoke any you don't recognize or no longer use.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Turn On App Lock" icon="fingerprint">
    Biometric App Lock protects your private messages if your phone is ever picked up by someone else.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
