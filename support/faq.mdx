Got a question? You're likely not the first to ask it. Below you'll find answers to the most common questions our community asks. If you don't find what you're looking for here, head over to [Contact Us](/support/contact-us) and our support team will be happy to help.

<Accordion title="What is AfuChat?">
  AfuChat is a mobile-first social super-app for Android designed to bring your digital life together in one place. It combines social feeds, direct messaging, voice and video calling, live streaming, a creator economy (ACoins, Nexa), and financial features (AfuPay) — all within a single app. Think of it as your community, wallet, and communication hub unified into one seamless experience.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is AfuChat free to download and use?">
  Yes — AfuChat is free to download and the core features (messaging, feed, following accounts, watching content) are available at no charge. AfuChat also offers **Premium** and **Prestige** subscription tiers that unlock additional features, enhanced visibility, and exclusive perks. Certain in-app transactions (such as sending ACoins or using AfuPay) involve real monetary value, but browsing and communicating are always free.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are ACoins?">
  **ACoins** are AfuChat's in-app virtual currency. You can use ACoins to:
  - Send gifts to creators during live streams or on their profiles
  - Unlock exclusive content
  - Boost your posts or profile visibility
  - Participate in certain in-app experiences

  ACoins can be purchased within the app. They are not the same as real-world currency and, unless otherwise stated, are non-refundable. Check the AfuPay section of the app for current purchase options and rates.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between Premium and Prestige?">
  AfuChat offers two paid subscription tiers above the free plan:

  **Premium** gives you access to enhanced features such as expanded messaging capabilities, additional customization options, and a badge that signals your support to the community.

  **Prestige** is the top-tier subscription and includes everything in Premium plus exclusive features, priority visibility, advanced analytics for creators, and premium support access.

  You can compare the exact benefits of each tier and subscribe within the app under **Me → Subscription** or during the sign-up flow.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is AfuChat available on iOS?">
  AfuChat is currently an **Android-first app** (version 2.2.8). An iOS version is planned — follow AfuChat's official channels and the announcement section in-app for updates on availability on Apple devices.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I change my @handle?">
  You can update your @handle at any time from **Settings → Manage Account**. Tap the handle field, type your new preferred username, and save the change.

  Keep in mind:
  - Handles must be unique across the platform
  - Changing your handle updates all profile links instantly
  - If you previously shared your profile link with others, those old links may no longer work — share your new handle after the change
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My messages aren't sending — what should I do?">
  If your messages are showing as pending or failing to send, here's what to check:

  1. **Check your internet connection** — AfuChat requires a data connection to send messages in real time.
  2. **AfuChat is offline-first** — if you're offline, your messages are queued automatically and will send as soon as your connection is restored. Look for a clock icon next to the message indicating it is queued.
  3. **Restart the app** — close AfuChat fully and reopen it.
  4. **Check the recipient's settings** — if they've restricted who can message them, your message may not go through.

  If none of these steps help, see our full [Connectivity Troubleshooting guide](/support/connectivity) or [contact support](/support/contact-us).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
  Account deletion is handled in **Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account**. After requesting deletion:

  - Your account is immediately deactivated and hidden from other users
  - A **30-day grace period** begins — if you sign back in during this time, the deletion is cancelled and your account is fully restored
  - After 30 days, all your data (profile, posts, messages, ACoins balance) is **permanently and irreversibly deleted**

  We recommend downloading your data before deleting — go to **Settings → Privacy → Data → Download Your Data** first.

  For a full step-by-step walkthrough, see [Account Settings → Danger Zone](/support/account-settings).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I earn Nexa?">
  **Nexa** is AfuChat's engagement and reward currency — it reflects your activity and contribution to the community. You can earn Nexa through actions such as:
  - Posting content and receiving engagement (likes, comments, shares)
  - Interacting with the community consistently
  - Completing in-app challenges and milestones
  - Referring new users to AfuChat

  Check the **Rewards** or **Nexa** section in the app for your current balance, available earning opportunities, and how Nexa can be redeemed or converted.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use AfuChat offline?">
  AfuChat is built with an **offline-first architecture**, meaning it's designed to keep working even when your connection drops. While offline:
  - Your existing conversations and cached content remain accessible
  - Messages you send are **queued locally** and delivered automatically once you're back online
  - Offline videos you've saved in **Settings → Storage → Offline Videos** can be watched without any connection

  Real-time features like live streams, calls, and loading new feed content require an active internet connection.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I report a bug?">
  We appreciate bug reports — they help make AfuChat better for everyone. To report a bug:

  1. Go to **Me tab → Help & Support → Report a Bug**
  2. Describe what happened, what you expected to happen, and the steps to reproduce the issue
  3. Attach any screenshots or crash logs if available (the app may offer to include them automatically)
  4. Submit — our engineering team reviews all bug reports

  For critical issues affecting your account, [contact support directly](/support/contact-us) so we can prioritize your case.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is AfuChat open source?">
  AfuChat is a proprietary application and is not open source at this time. If you're interested in building on top of AfuChat's platform or exploring partnership integrations, please reach out via **afuchat.com** for business and developer inquiries.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
  There are several ways to reach the AfuChat support team:

  - **In-app:** Go to **Me tab → Help & Support**, or open **afuchat://support** directly
  - **Submit a ticket:** Use the in-app ticketing system to describe your issue and track its progress
  - **Web:** Visit **afuchat.com** for additional contact and business inquiry options

  Standard tickets are typically responded to within **24–48 hours**. Safety-related issues receive priority handling. For full details, see our [Contact Us](/support/contact-us) page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What countries are supported for AfuPay?">
  AfuPay, AfuChat's in-app payment feature, is available in a growing number of countries. Supported regions are listed within the **AfuPay** section of the app, and availability may vary depending on local regulations and payment infrastructure.

  If AfuPay is not yet available in your country, you can still use all other AfuChat features. Stay tuned to AfuChat's official announcements for updates on new country launches.

  <Note>
    If you're experiencing issues with a specific AfuPay transaction, please include your transaction ID or reference number when contacting support — this helps us locate your case quickly.
  </Note>
</Accordion>
