AfuChat gives you two powerful tools for building spaces around shared interests: **Communities** for open, two-way group discussions at any scale, and **Channels** for one-to-many broadcasts where only admins post and followers receive. Whether you're a creator building an audience, a business keeping customers informed, or a group of friends with a shared passion, there's a space designed for you.

---

## Communities

### What Is a Community?

A Community is a group space where every member can read and send messages, react, and participate in discussions. Communities are ideal for fan groups, study circles, professional networks, local interest groups, and anything else that benefits from open conversation among many people.

### Joining a Community

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Via invite link">
    Tap an `afuchat://group/[id]` deep link shared by a member or admin. AfuChat opens automatically and displays the Community preview screen — tap **Join** to become a member.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Via QR code">
    Open the AfuChat scanner (tap the QR icon on the home screen or in a Community's share menu) and scan the Community's QR code. You're taken straight to the Join screen.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Via direct invite">
    An existing member or admin can add you directly. You'll receive an in-app notification with an **Accept** or **Decline** option.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Via Discover">
    Find Communities through the **Discover** tab. Tap a Community card and then tap **Join** on the Community profile page.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Creating a Community

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Communities tab">
    Tap **Communities** in the bottom navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the + button">
    Tap the **+** icon in the top-right corner of the Communities screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your Community">
    - Enter a **Community name** (choose something clear and searchable).
    - Add a **description** so people know what your community is about.
    - Upload a **community photo** — a square image works best.
    - Set the community to **Public** (anyone can find and join) or **Private** (invite only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your first members">
    Share the auto-generated invite link or QR code, or manually invite contacts from your AfuChat list. Your community is live as soon as it's created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Community Roles

Every member of a Community has one of three roles:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card icon="crown" title="Owner">
    The person who created the Community. Has full control: can promote/demote admins, delete the community, and access all admin tools. There is one owner per community.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="shield-halved" title="Admin">
    Trusted members promoted by the Owner. Admins can invite and remove members, pin messages, post announcements, and manage the invite link.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="user" title="Member">
    All other participants. Members can read and send messages, react, reply, and share media in line with community rules.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Admin Tools

<Accordion title="Invite link management">
  Go to **Community Info → Invite Link** to copy, share, or **reset** the link. Resetting the link invalidates all previous links immediately — useful if you need to stop new joins from an old shared link.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Removing members">
  Open **Community Info → Members**, long-press a member's name, and tap **Remove from Community**. Removed members can only rejoin if re-invited by an admin.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Pinning messages">
  Long-press any message inside the Community and tap **Pin Message**. Pinned messages appear at the top of the chat for all members to see. Admins can pin up to 3 messages simultaneously.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Announcements">
  Admins can send a special **Announcement** message that notifies all community members regardless of their notification settings. Use this sparingly for genuinely important updates.
</Accordion>

### Paid Communities

You can lock your Community behind a subscription so that only paying members can join and participate.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Community Settings">
    Go to **Community Info → Settings** (visible to Owner and Admins only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Paid Access">
    Toggle **Paid Community** on and set your subscription price in **ACoins** — AfuChat's in-app currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save**. From this point, new members must pay the ACoins subscription fee before they can view or participate in the Community. Existing members retain access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Once Paid Access is enabled, any new invite links will direct users to a payment screen before they can join. Make sure your community description clearly communicates the value members get for their subscription.
</Warning>

## Channels

### What Is a Channel?

A Channel is a broadcast-only space. Only the Channel's admins can post; everyone else is a **follower** who receives the posts in their feed. Channels are ideal for official announcements, newsletters, product updates, creator dispatches, and any situation where you want a clear one-to-many signal without conversation noise.

### Following a Channel

Tap an `afuchat://channel/[id]` deep link or find Channels in the **Discover → Featured Channels** section. On the Channel profile page, tap **Follow** — new posts will appear in your Channels feed.

### Creating a Channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the deep link shortcut">
    Open `afuchat://channel/create` in any browser or tap it from a shared link — AfuChat will open directly on the Channel creation screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your Channel">
    - Enter a **Channel name** and write a short description.
    - Add a **Channel photo**.
    - Choose whether the Channel is **Public** (searchable and joinable by anyone) or **Private** (invite-only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Tap **Create Channel**. Your Channel is live and ready to receive its first broadcast post.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Share your Channel's `afuchat://channel/[id]` deep link across your other social profiles to drive followers to your AfuChat Channel.
</Tip>

## Deep Links Reference

Use these deep links to direct people straight to a Community or Channel, in marketing copy, QR codes, or shared links:

| Destination | Deep link format |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Join a Community | `afuchat://group/[community-id]` |
| View a Channel | `afuchat://channel/[channel-id]` |
| Create a Channel | `afuchat://channel/create` |

<Note>
  Deep links open AfuChat automatically if it is installed on the device. If AfuChat is not installed, direct users to the Play Store to download it first.
</Note>

## Communities vs Channels at a Glance

| Feature | Communities | Channels |
|---------|------------|---------|
| Who can post | All members | Admins only |
| Member interaction | Two-way discussion | Read / react only |
| Suitable for | Group discussions, fan spaces | Announcements, broadcasts |
| Paid access | ✅ ACoins subscription | ❌ |
| Roles | Owner, Admin, Member | Admin, Follower |
| Join via | Link, QR, invite, Discover | Link, Discover, Follow |
| Deep link | `afuchat://group/[id]` | `afuchat://channel/[id]` |
