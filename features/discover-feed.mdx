The Discover tab is AfuChat's window to the wider world. Instead of showing you only content from people you already follow, Discover uses your interests, location settings, and activity to surface posts, videos, trending topics, and new accounts you didn't know you were looking for. The more you use it, the smarter it gets.

## How Personalisation Works

Discover curates your feed using three core signals:

<Accordion title="Interests">
  When you set up your AfuChat profile, you choose a set of interest categories (e.g. *Music*, *Technology*, *Sport*, *Food*, *Travel*). Discover weights content and accounts in these categories more heavily. You can update your interests at any time in your profile settings.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Country & Region">
  AfuChat uses the **country and region** you set in your profile — not your device's GPS — to surface locally relevant trending content and accounts. This means your Discover feed reflects what's popular where you are, not just globally.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Your activity">
  Posts you engage with (likes, comments, shares, saves, and watch time on Shorts) train the algorithm over time. The more you interact, the more refined and relevant your feed becomes.
</Accordion>

<Note>
  AfuChat uses your **profile-set** country and region for personalisation, not real-time GPS data. To see content from a different region, update your profile location in **Settings → Profile → Location**.
</Note>

## Updating Your Feed Preferences

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your profile">
    Tap your avatar in the bottom navigation bar to open your profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open profile settings">
    Tap the **edit / settings icon** at the top of your profile screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update interests">
    Scroll to the **Interests** section and tap to add or remove interest categories. Changes take effect in your Discover feed within a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update country and region">
    In the same settings screen, tap **Location** to change your country and sub-region. This updates your regional trending content and People You May Know suggestions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Controlling What You See

You don't have to accept every piece of content the algorithm serves. AfuChat gives you direct controls to fine-tune your Discover experience.

### 'Not Interested' on a Post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Long-press or tap the menu">
    On any post in Discover, long-press the post card or tap the **⋮ (more options)** icon in the top-right corner of the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Not Interested'">
    Select **Not Interested** from the menu. AfuChat immediately removes that post from your feed and uses the signal to show you less similar content going forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use **Not Interested** liberally when you first start using Discover — a few taps of feedback quickly sharpens your feed to things you genuinely care about.
</Tip>

## Searching in Discover

The search bar at the top of the Discover tab lets you search across all public AfuChat content:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Posts & Hashtags">
    Type a keyword or hashtag (with or without the `#` symbol) to see matching posts, sorted by recency and relevance. Tap any hashtag result to open the full hashtag feed.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="People">
    Search by name or username to find specific accounts. Profile cards in results let you follow directly from the search page.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Communities & Channels">
    Search by community or channel name to find groups to join. Results show member count and a brief description to help you decide.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## AfuShorts Tab in Discover

The **Shorts** tab within Discover is a dedicated full-screen vertical video browsing experience. Swipe up through an endless feed of short-form videos filtered to your interests — no following required.

<Info>
  The AfuShorts tab inside Discover and the standalone AfuShorts tab in the navigation bar show the same algorithmic feed. Use whichever entry point you prefer.
</Info>
