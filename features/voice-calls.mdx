AfuChat lets you move from a text conversation to a live voice call in a single tap — no phone number required, no third-party app needed. Calls connect directly between you and the other person for low-latency, private audio, and the audio quality adjusts automatically to your network conditions.

## Starting a Voice Call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a 1-on-1 chat">
    Voice calls are available in any direct message conversation. Open the chat with the person you want to call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the phone icon">
    Tap the **phone icon (📞)** in the chat header, at the top-right of the screen. AfuChat will start dialling immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the other person to answer">
    You'll see a calling screen with the contact's avatar while AfuChat rings their device. The call connects as soon as they accept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Voice calls are currently available in **1-on-1 chats only**. Group voice calls are not yet supported.
</Note>

## The Call Screen

AfuChat uses a glass-design call interface that keeps controls minimal and the focus on your conversation.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card icon="circle-user" title="Contact Avatar">
    Your contact's profile photo is displayed front and centre. A subtle animation indicates when audio is being transmitted.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="microphone-slash" title="Mute">
    Tap the **mute button** to silence your microphone. The icon turns red and the other person sees a muted indicator. Tap again to unmute.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="volume-high" title="Speaker">
    Toggle the **speaker button** to switch between earpiece and loudspeaker mode — useful when you want hands-free audio.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="phone-xmark" title="End Call">
    Tap the **red end-call button** to hang up. The call duration is shown on the call ended screen and saved to your call history.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Receiving an Incoming Call

When someone calls you on AfuChat, you'll receive a full-screen incoming call modal with the caller's name and avatar, regardless of whether the app is open or in the background.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="App is open">
    An incoming call modal slides up over your current screen. Tap **Accept** (green) to answer or **Decline** (red) to reject the call. The caller is notified immediately either way.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="App in background / screen locked">
    A lock screen or heads-up notification appears with **Accept** and **Decline** buttons — you can answer without even unlocking your phone.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  Make sure AfuChat has notification permissions enabled so you never miss an incoming call. Go to your device **Settings → Apps → AfuChat → Notifications** to check.
</Tip>

## Missed Calls & Notifications

If you miss a call, AfuChat sends you a **missed call notification** with a tap-to-call-back shortcut. The missed call also appears in your **Call History** (see below) flagged in red so it's easy to spot.

## Call History

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Contacts tab">
    Tap **Contacts** in the bottom navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Call History">
    Tap the **phone / clock icon** at the top of the Contacts screen to switch to the Call History view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and call back">
    Each entry shows the contact name, call direction (incoming/outgoing/missed), date, and duration. Tap any entry to call that person back instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Call Quality & Network Adaptation

AfuChat voice calls use peer-to-peer audio technology with the Opus audio codec, which is designed to deliver clear voice at low data usage (approximately 20 kbps). The codec adapts in real time to your available bandwidth — you won't experience sudden drop-outs; quality degrades gracefully instead.

<Accordion title="Tips for the best call quality">
  - **Use Wi-Fi when possible** — a stable Wi-Fi connection gives the most consistent results.
  - **Move closer to your router** — weak Wi-Fi signal causes packet loss and choppy audio.
  - **On mobile data, 4G/LTE is ideal** — calls work on 3G but audio quality may vary.
  - **Close background apps** — apps streaming video or downloading large files compete for bandwidth.
  - **Use earphones** — reduces echo and background noise picked up by the microphone.
  - **Find a quiet space** — AfuChat uses the device's built-in noise suppression, but a quieter environment always helps.
</Accordion>

<Warning>
  If the call quality is poor, both you and your contact are on the same P2P connection — ask the other person to check their network as well.
</Warning>
