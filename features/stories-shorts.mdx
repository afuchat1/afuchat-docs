AfuChat gives you two distinct ways to share and discover visual content: **Stories** for ephemeral, personal moments that disappear after 24 hours, and **AfuShorts** for a never-ending feed of algorithmically curated short-form videos from creators across the platform. Both live inside the same app — no switching required.

## Stories

### What Are Stories?

Stories are photos or short videos that you post for your followers or friends to see. They stay visible for **24 hours** and then disappear automatically — no clean-up needed. Stories are perfect for in-the-moment updates, behind-the-scenes content, or anything you don't want to keep permanently on your profile.

### Creating a Story

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Camera tab">
    Tap the **Camera tab** (or the **+ Story** button at the top of your feed). This opens AfuChat's built-in camera.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture or upload">
    Take a new photo or video using the shutter button, or tap the **gallery icon** in the bottom-left to pick an existing image or clip from your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decorate your Story">
    Use the editing toolbar to enhance your Story before posting:
    - **Text** — add captions in your choice of font, colour, and size.
    - **Stickers** — browse the sticker library including emojis, location tags, and time stamps.
    - **Draw** — freehand drawing tools for a personal touch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post">
    Tap **Your Story** to share with all your followers, or choose specific people for a closer-audience share. Your Story goes live instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Viewing Stories

Stories from people you follow appear as circular avatars with a coloured ring along the top of your home feed. Tap any ring to watch that person's Story. Tap the right side of the screen to skip to the next frame; tap the left side to go back.

<Tip>
  Swipe up while watching a Story to reply directly to that person via a private message — great for reacting without a public comment.
</Tip>

### Story Views & Analytics

As the creator, you can see exactly who viewed your Story.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your own Story">
    Tap your profile avatar with the ring at the top of the feed to open your active Story.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swipe up">
    Swipe up from the bottom of the screen to reveal the **viewer list** — every account that has watched your Story is shown along with timestamps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Story Expiry

<Info>
  Stories expire automatically **24 hours** after they are posted. Once expired, they are removed from your followers' view and from your own Story archive. There is no way to extend a Story's lifespan after it has been published.
</Info>

## AfuShorts

### What Are AfuShorts?

AfuShorts is AfuChat's short-form vertical video feed — think of it as an endless stream of creative clips from creators you follow and new creators the algorithm thinks you'll enjoy. Every video is full-screen, plays automatically, and is tailored to your interests.

### Browsing AfuShorts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to AfuShorts">
    Tap the **AfuShorts tab** (clapperboard icon) in the bottom navigation bar, or find it as a dedicated tab within the **Discover** screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swipe to explore">
    Swipe **up** to move to the next video; swipe **down** to go back to the previous one. Videos loop automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interact with content">
    Use the action bar on the right side of each video to engage:
    - ❤️ **Like** — double-tap the screen or tap the heart icon.
    - 💬 **Comment** — tap the speech bubble to open the comment thread.
    - ↗️ **Share** — send the Short to a chat, share to your Story, or copy the link.
    - 🔖 **Save** — tap the bookmark icon to add the video to your saved collections.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Creator Distribution

AfuShorts uses AfuChat's content algorithm to surface your videos to new audiences based on topic, engagement, and region — you don't need a large following for your content to reach people. The more engaging your Shorts are, the wider the algorithm distributes them.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card icon="chart-line" title="Algorithmic Reach">
    Your Shorts are shown to viewers beyond your existing followers based on interest matching, watch time, and engagement signals.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="globe" title="Regional Discovery">
    Content is distributed based on country and region, so local creators naturally surface to nearby audiences first.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Creating a Short

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the creation screen">
    Tap the **+ icon** on the AfuShorts tab, or go to **Camera → Shorts mode**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record or upload your video">
    Record directly in the app (up to approximately **60 seconds**) using the hold-to-record button, or upload an existing video from your gallery. You can stitch multiple clips together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a caption and hashtags">
    Write a caption that describes your video. Add relevant **#hashtags** to help the algorithm and searching users find your content — you can add up to 10 hashtags.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post your Short">
    Tap **Post** to publish. Your Short will be reviewed against community guidelines and then distributed on the AfuShorts feed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  AfuShorts must comply with AfuChat's Community Guidelines. Content that violates these guidelines will be removed and may result in posting restrictions on your account.
</Warning>

## Stories vs AfuShorts at a Glance

| Feature | Stories | AfuShorts |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Lifespan | 24 hours | Permanent (until deleted) |
| Format | Photo or short video | Short-form vertical video |
| Audience | Your followers / friends | Algorithmic — anyone on AfuChat |
| Discovery | Manual (tap avatar) | Auto-served in feed |
| Interactions | Views, replies | Likes, comments, shares, saves |
| Creator analytics | Viewer list | Views, likes, comments, shares |
