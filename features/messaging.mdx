AfuChat's messaging experience puts everything you need in one place — from quick one-on-one chats to large group conversations, with rich media support and a fully personalised look and feel for every conversation. Whether you're online or off, your messages are always with you.

## Starting a New Chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Compose screen">
    Tap the **compose icon** (pencil) in the top-right corner of your Chats tab, or use the search bar to find a contact by name or username.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a recipient">
    Select a contact from your list, or enter a username manually. To start a **group chat**, tap **New Group** and add multiple contacts at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your first message">
    Type in the message bar and tap **Send**, or choose one of the attachment options to jump straight to media or voice notes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Direct Messages vs Group Chats

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card icon="user" title="Direct Messages">
    A private 1-on-1 conversation between you and one other person. Full read receipts, reactions, voice calls, and chat appearance customisation are all available.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="users" title="Group Chats">
    Bring multiple contacts into a shared space. Add a group name and photo, assign admins, and enjoy the same rich messaging features as direct chats.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Message Types

You can send far more than plain text in any chat. Tap the **attachment icon (📎)** next to the message bar to reveal your options:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Media & Files">
    - **Images & Video** — share photos and clips directly from your gallery.
    - **GIFs** — search the built-in GIF library and tap to send.
    - **Files** — attach documents, PDFs, or any file from your device storage.
    - **Location** — drop a live or static pin so others know exactly where you are.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Voice Notes">
    Hold down the **microphone icon** to record a voice note. Slide left to cancel, or release to send. Recipients can play the note inline — no need to open a separate player.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Text">
    Type in the message bar and use the formatting toolbar to apply **bold**, *italic*, or `monospace` styles. Emoji picker is always one tap away.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Reactions & Replies

**Reacting to a message** — long-press any message bubble to open the reaction picker. Choose an emoji to add your reaction; it appears below the message for everyone in the chat.

**Replying to a message** — swipe right on any message to quote it as a reply. The original message is embedded above your response so the thread stays clear, even in busy group chats.

<Tip>
  In a group chat, replying to a specific message is the best way to keep conversations organised when multiple topics are happening at once.
</Tip>

## Searching Inside a Chat

Tap the **search icon (🔍)** in the chat header to search through the entire message history of that conversation. Results are highlighted in context so you can jump straight to the relevant point in the thread.

## Read Receipts

AfuChat shows delivery and read status beneath every message you send:

| Indicator | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| Single tick | Message sent to AfuChat servers |
| Double tick | Message delivered to the recipient's device |
| Double tick (filled) | Message has been read by the recipient |

<Note>
  Read receipts are visible in 1-on-1 chats. In group chats, you can long-press a message and tap **Info** to see which members have seen it.
</Note>

## Offline Mode

AfuChat caches all your messages locally on your device using an on-device database, so you can read and compose messages even without an internet connection.

- **Reading** — your full conversation history is available offline.
- **Sending** — messages you send while offline are queued locally and delivered automatically the moment your connection is restored.
- **Media** — previously loaded images and voice notes remain accessible offline.

<Info>
  You'll see a small **pending** indicator on queued messages. Once delivered, the indicator updates to the standard tick system automatically.
</Info>

## Chat Appearance

Personalise the look of any conversation to make it feel uniquely yours.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open chat settings">
    Inside any chat, tap the contact name or group name at the top of the screen to open **Chat Info**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Appearance'">
    Select **Appearance** from the Chat Info menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customise">
    - Choose a **bubble colour** for your outgoing messages.
    - Pick a **chat background** — a solid colour, gradient, or image from your gallery.
    - Changes apply only to this conversation; other chats are unaffected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Set a different background for your closest contacts so you know at a glance which conversation you're in.
</Tip>

## Chat Folders

Keep your inbox tidy by organising conversations into folders.

<Accordion title="Creating and managing folders">
  1. On the **Chats** tab, tap the **folder icon** or go to **Settings → Chat Folders**.
  2. Tap **New Folder** and give it a name (e.g. *Work*, *Family*, *Gaming*).
  3. Add existing chats to the folder — you can include both direct messages and group chats.
  4. Folders appear as tabs at the top of your Chats screen for one-tap access.
  5. To edit or delete a folder, long-press it and choose the appropriate option.
</Accordion>

## Editing & Deleting Messages

- **Edit** — long-press a sent message and tap **Edit**. You can correct the text and resend; recipients see an *edited* label on the message.
- **Delete for me** — removes the message from your view only.
- **Delete for everyone** — removes the message from all participants' views (available within a time window after sending).

<Warning>
  Deleted messages cannot be recovered. Use the **Edit** option if you just need to fix a typo.
</Warning>

## Forwarding Messages

Long-press any message and tap **Forward**. Select one or more chats or contacts from the list, then tap **Send**. Forwarded messages display a small *Forwarded* label to keep context clear.
