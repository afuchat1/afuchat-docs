Creating an AfuChat account gives you access to the full super-app — your social feed, messaging, Shorts, AfuPay, AfuMarket, and everything else AfuChat has to offer. The sign-up process takes just a minute or two, and you have two ways to get started: with your email address or with your existing Google account.

## Before You Sign Up

There are a few things worth knowing before you create your account:

- You must be **13 years of age or older** to create an AfuChat account.
- Your **@handle** (username) must be unique across the entire AfuChat platform. Choose it carefully — it becomes part of your profile URL and how people find and mention you.
- Your **email address** is used for account verification, password recovery, and important security notifications.
- A **phone number** is optional at sign-up, but adding one later helps people you know find you on AfuChat through contact discovery.

<Info>
  AfuChat's welcome experience is personalised from the moment you join. As soon as you complete sign-up, **AfuAI** — AfuChat's built-in AI assistant — will automatically send you a welcome message to help you get oriented.
</Info>

## Option 1: Sign Up with Email and Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AfuChat and tap 'Sign Up'">
    Launch the AfuChat app on your Android device. On the welcome screen, tap **Sign Up** to begin creating a new account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Type in a valid email address that you have access to. AfuChat will send a verification email to this address, so make sure it is one you can check right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a strong password">
    Choose a password for your account. Your password should be at least 8 characters long and include a mix of letters and numbers for security. AfuChat does not display your password to anyone, including AfuChat staff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your @handle">
    Pick a unique username — your **@handle**. This is how people will find, mention, and share your profile across AfuChat. Handles are **lowercase only** and may contain **letters, numbers, and underscores** (`_`). No spaces or special characters are allowed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your display name">
    Your **display name** is the name shown on your profile and in feeds. Unlike your handle, it can contain spaces, capital letters, and most Unicode characters — so feel free to use your real name or a creative identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    AfuChat will send a verification link to the email address you provided. Open that email and tap the verification link. You will be redirected back to the app once your email is confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile basics">
    After verification, you will be prompted to add a profile photo, select your country and interests, and optionally add a phone number. You can skip these steps and complete them later from your profile settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Option 2: Sign Up with Google

If you prefer not to manage a separate password, you can create and access your AfuChat account using **Google Sign-In (OAuth)**. This links your AfuChat account to your Google identity securely.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap 'Continue with Google' on the welcome screen">
    On the AfuChat welcome screen, tap the **Continue with Google** button instead of the email sign-up form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your Google account">
    Android will display a list of Google accounts available on your device. Tap the account you want to use for AfuChat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant permissions">
    AfuChat requests access to your name and email address from your Google account to pre-fill your profile. Review the permissions and tap **Allow** to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your @handle">
    Even with Google Sign-In, you still need to choose a unique **@handle** for AfuChat. Follow the same rules: lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish profile setup">
    Complete the quick onboarding flow — add a photo, pick your interests, and optionally add your phone number. Then you are ready to explore AfuChat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you sign up with Google, you can still add a password to your account later from **Me → Settings → Security**. This gives you a backup way to sign in if you ever switch Google accounts or devices.
</Tip>

## Choosing Your @Handle

Your @handle is one of the most important decisions you make when joining AfuChat. Here is what you need to know:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Unique Forever" icon="fingerprint">
    No two people on AfuChat can share the same @handle. Once you claim it, it is yours. If the handle you want is already taken, AfuChat will suggest available alternatives.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Format Rules" icon="at">
    Handles must be lowercase and contain only letters (a–z), numbers (0–9), and underscores (_). They cannot start with a number or underscore. Minimum 3 characters, maximum 30 characters.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Used for Mentions" icon="at">
    Other people mention you in posts, comments, and messages using your @handle. Make it something memorable and easy to type.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Changing Your Handle" icon="pencil">
    You can change your @handle after sign-up from your profile settings, but do so carefully — any existing mentions or links using your old handle will not automatically update.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Signing In to an Existing Account

If you already have an AfuChat account and are signing back in on a new device:

- **Email/password accounts:** Tap **Sign In**, enter your email and password, and you are in.
- **Google accounts:** Tap **Continue with Google** and select the same Google account you used to register. No password needed.

<Warning>
  If you cannot remember your password, tap **Forgot Password** on the sign-in screen. AfuChat will send a password reset link to your registered email address. If you no longer have access to that email, contact AfuChat support through **afuchat.com** for account recovery assistance.
</Warning>

## After Sign-Up: Your First Steps

Once your account is live, here is what happens automatically and what you can do next:

- **AfuAI sends you a welcome message** — check your Chats tab to find it. AfuAI can help you find people to follow, explain features, and more.
- **Your feed starts populating** — based on the interests you selected, AfuChat surfaces relevant posts and creators for you to explore.
- **You earn your first XP** — completing profile setup and early actions earns you XP points that level up your AfuChat profile over time.

Ready to set up the rest of your profile? Head to [Your Profile](/getting-started/your-profile) to add your avatar, banner, bio, and more.
