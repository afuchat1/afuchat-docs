AfuChat is designed to be intuitive from the moment you open it. The app is organised around a bottom navigation bar that gives you instant access to every major section of the super-app. Whether you are scrolling your personalised feed, catching up on messages, watching Shorts, or exploring mini-apps, everything is always one tap away.

## Main Navigation Bar

The AfuChat navigation bar runs along the bottom of the screen and contains six tabs. Each one opens a distinct part of the app with its own purpose and set of features.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Home" icon="house">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Chats" icon="message">
    Your messaging hub. Send direct messages to individuals, participate in group chats, and find the welcome message from AfuAI that arrived when you first joined. Chats support text, media, voice notes, and ACoins tips.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shorts" icon="film">
    Short-form vertical video, built into AfuChat. Scroll through an endless feed of creator videos from across Africa and the global diaspora. Record and publish your own Shorts directly from this tab.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Discover" icon="compass">
    Explore beyond your existing follows. Discover surfaces trending posts, suggested creators, popular topics, and community channels based on your interests and location settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Me" icon="circle-user">
    Your profile and personal hub. View your posts, followers, following, XP level, and ACoins balance. Access all app settings — privacy, security, notifications, payments, and more — from this tab.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Apps" icon="grid-2">
    The mini-apps launcher. Access AfuChat's first-party services like AfuAI, AfuPay, and AfuMarket, plus any third-party mini-apps available in your region — all without leaving AfuChat.

## Home Feed

The **Home** tab is where you spend most of your time on AfuChat. It shows you a chronological and algorithmic mix of:

- Posts and updates from people you follow.
- Content related to the interests you set on your profile.
- Sponsored and promoted content from AfuChat partners.
- Community highlights and trending posts surfaced by AfuChat's recommendation engine.

<Tip>
  To get the most relevant Home feed, make sure your **interests** are fully set up in your profile. The more specific you are about what you care about, the better AfuChat can personalise what you see.
</Tip>

## Chats

The **Chats** tab is your full-featured messaging centre. You can:

- Start **one-on-one direct messages** with any AfuChat user you follow (or who follows you, depending on privacy settings).
- Create and participate in **group chats** with multiple people at once.
- Send **ACoins** as a tip directly inside a conversation.
- Share posts, Shorts, and profile links into any chat thread.
- Receive and interact with **AfuAI** as a chat contact — ask it questions, get content ideas, or request help navigating the app.

<Note>
  When you first join AfuChat, **AfuAI automatically sends you a welcome message** in your Chats tab. Open it to get personalised tips for setting up your account and discovering your first community connections.
</Note>

## Shorts

AfuChat **Shorts** is the short-form video experience built natively into the super-app. You can browse a full-screen vertical video feed with content from creators across the AfuChat community. Interact with Shorts by:

- **Liking and commenting** on videos you enjoy.
- **Sharing** a Short to your feed, a chat, or externally via link.
- **Tipping** a creator ACoins directly from the video.
- **Following** creators you discover while watching.

To create your own Short, tap the camera icon within the Shorts tab and record or upload a video. You can add captions, choose a cover frame, and publish to your followers.

## Discover

The **Discover** tab is your gateway to content and people outside your existing follow graph. Use it to:

- **Search across all of AfuChat** — people, posts, channels, topics, and mini-apps.
- **Browse trending content** in your region or globally.
- **Find community channels** around specific topics, interests, or locations.
- **Discover suggested creators** that AfuChat thinks you will enjoy based on your profile and activity.

<Tip>
  The search bar in Discover is powerful — you can search by @handle to find a specific person, by keyword to find posts on a topic, or by channel name to find a community to join.
</Tip>

## Me (Your Profile Hub)

The **Me** tab is your personal command centre on AfuChat. From here you can:

- View and edit your public profile.
- Browse your own posts, liked content, and saved items.
- Check your **XP level** and **ACoins balance**.
- Access **Settings** for full control over privacy, security, notifications, language, payment methods, and account management.
- See your **follower and following** counts and manage both lists.

## Apps (Mini-Apps Launcher)

The **Apps** tab is what makes AfuChat a true super-app. It launches a panel of services — both AfuChat's own and third-party — that you can use directly without leaving the AfuChat environment.

## Notifications

AfuChat keeps you connected to what matters through a unified notification system. You receive notifications for:

- **Social activity** — likes, comments, mentions, and new followers.
- **Messages** — new direct messages and group chat activity.
- **Tipping** — when someone sends you ACoins.
- **App updates** — new mini-apps, feature launches, and AfuChat announcements.
- **Community** — activity in channels and communities you belong to.

Access your notifications by tapping the bell icon, typically found in the top-right area of the Home or Discover tab. You can fine-tune which notifications you receive in **Me → Settings → Notifications**.

<Tip>
  If you find AfuChat notifications overwhelming at first, start by disabling categories you care less about. Most users find that keeping **Messages** and **Mentions** on while turning off broader social activity strikes a good balance.
</Tip>

## Search

AfuChat's search is available from the Discover tab and finds content across the entire platform:

| Search Type  | What You Can Find                                    |
|-------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| **People**  | Search by @handle or display name to find any user   |
| **Posts**   | Find posts by keyword, hashtag, or topic             |
| **Channels**| Discover community channels organised around topics  |
| **Mini-Apps**| Find specific AfuChat apps and services by name     |

Search results are filtered by relevance and your location/language settings, so you see the most useful results for your context first.

Now that you know your way around, dive in — your AfuChat community is waiting. Start by following a few people from Discover, check out what is trending in Shorts, and let AfuAI help you get settled in through the Chats tab.
