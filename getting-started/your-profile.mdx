Your AfuChat profile is your identity on the platform — it is the first thing people see when they visit your page, and it shapes how the app personalises your experience. Taking a few minutes to fill it in properly means better content recommendations, easier discovery by people you know, and a stronger first impression on anyone who finds you through search or a shared post.

## Accessing Your Profile

To open your profile, tap the **Me** tab at the bottom of the AfuChat app. From there, tap the **Edit Profile** button to access all of your profile fields. You can update any of these at any time.

## Profile Fields

### Display Name

Your **display name** is the name shown prominently at the top of your profile card and alongside your posts in feeds. It can include spaces, capital letters, and most characters — this is where you put the name you actually want people to call you. It does not need to be unique.

### @Handle

Your **@handle** is your unique username on AfuChat and forms part of your profile link. It appears below your display name on your profile. You can change it in profile settings, but note that existing mentions and links using your old handle will not redirect automatically.

<Note>
  Your @handle must be lowercase and contain only letters, numbers, and underscores. See [Create Your Account](/getting-started/create-account) for the full rules on handles.
</Note>

### Profile Photo (Avatar)

Your **profile photo** (avatar) appears on your profile, in search results, alongside your posts, and in chat threads. Upload a clear, recognisable photo or image to make yourself easy to find and identify. AfuChat supports standard image formats (JPG, PNG, WebP).

<Tip>
  Square images work best for profile photos. AfuChat crops avatars into a circle in most display contexts, so center your subject and avoid important details near the edges.
</Tip>

### Profile Banner

Your **banner** is the wide image that stretches across the top of your profile page behind your avatar. Use it to express your personality, showcase your work, or reinforce your brand if you are a creator or business. A good banner makes your profile page feel complete and professional.

### Bio

Your **bio** is a short text description that appears on your profile below your display name and handle. Use it to introduce yourself, describe what you post about, or share a memorable line about who you are. Keep it concise — people scan bios quickly.

### Website URL

If you have a personal website, portfolio, business page, or link-in-bio page, add it to your **website URL** field. It appears as a tappable link on your profile so followers can easily find your work outside of AfuChat.

### Country and Region

Setting your **country** and **region** helps AfuChat surface locally relevant content in your feed and connect you with communities near you. These fields also help AfuChat tailor features like AfuPay to your location.

### Interests

Your **interests** are one of the most impactful profile fields for your day-to-day AfuChat experience. The interests you select are used directly to personalise your home feed, your Discover recommendations, and the creators AfuChat suggests you follow. Choose the topics that genuinely matter to you for the best results.

### Date of Birth and Gender

These fields are used to personalise your experience and, in some regions, to comply with age-appropriate content regulations. Your date of birth is not displayed publicly on your profile. Your gender setting is optional and visible only according to your privacy preferences.

### Phone Number

Adding a **phone number** to your profile is optional. If you choose to add one, it enables **contact discovery** — AfuChat can identify when people in your phone contacts are also on AfuChat and suggest them as people you may know.

<Info>
  You can control whether your phone number is used for contact discovery at any time in **Me → Settings → Privacy**. Removing the number from your profile disables this feature.
</Info>

## Privacy Settings

### Public vs. Private Profile

AfuChat gives you full control over who can see your content and follow you.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Public Profile" icon="globe">
    Anyone on AfuChat — and in some cases across the open web — can view your posts, follow you, and engage with your content. This is the default setting and is recommended for creators and community builders.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Private Profile" icon="lock">
    Only people you approve as followers can see your posts and full profile. When your profile is private, follow requests must be accepted before someone can access your content.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Switch between public and private at any time by going to **Me → Settings → Privacy → Private Account** and toggling the setting.

### Online Status

The **Show Online Status** toggle controls whether other users can see when you are currently active in AfuChat. When enabled, a green indicator may appear on your avatar in chat and profile views. Turn it off from **Me → Settings → Privacy** if you prefer to browse privately.

### Tipping

**Tipping** allows other AfuChat users to send you **ACoins** directly from your profile as a show of appreciation for your content. When tipping is enabled, a tip button appears on your profile. You can toggle tipping on or off from **Me → Settings → Wallet & Payments** at any time.

<Tip>
  If you are a creator or post regularly, enabling tipping is a quick way to open up an additional income stream directly through your AfuChat presence — no separate payment setup required.
</Tip>

## Verification Badge

AfuChat offers a **verification badge** for organisations, public figures, and recognised brands. The badge appears next to your display name across the platform and signals to other users that your account is authentic.

<Note>
  Verification is currently available for **organisations and public figures**. Individual user verification is not available at this time. If you represent an organisation and want to apply, visit **afuchat.com** for more information on the verification process.
</Note>

## XP and ACoins on Your Profile

Your profile also displays two key account metrics:

- **XP (Experience Points)** — earned by being active on AfuChat: posting, engaging, completing profile setup, and participating in community events. XP contributes to your profile level and community standing.
- **ACoins** — AfuChat's in-app digital currency. ACoins can be earned through tipping, community rewards, and AfuPay activity, and spent within the AfuChat ecosystem.

Both values are visible on your profile and update in real time as you use the app.
