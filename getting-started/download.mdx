AfuChat is a mobile-first Android app, and getting it on your device takes less than two minutes. Whether you install it from the Google Play Store or download the APK directly from the AfuChat website, you will be up and running — and ready to join the community — in just a few steps.

## System Requirements

Before you install, make sure your Android device meets the minimum requirements for AfuChat to run smoothly.

| Requirement          | Minimum                          |
|---------------------|----------------------------------|
| **Operating System** | Android 5.0 (Lollipop) or higher |
| **API Level**        | API 24 or higher                 |
| **Storage**          | ~100 MB free space (app + cache) |
| **Internet**         | Active mobile data or Wi-Fi      |

<Note>
  AfuChat is currently available for **Android only**. An iOS version is in development. Stay tuned to the official AfuChat community channel for release announcements.
</Note>

## Install from Google Play Store

The Google Play Store is the easiest and recommended way to install AfuChat. Updates are delivered automatically, so you always have the latest version.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Google Play Store">
    On your Android device, open the **Google Play Store** app. Make sure you are signed in with your Google account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for AfuChat">
    Tap the search bar at the top and type **AfuChat**. Look for the result with the official AfuChat logo and the publisher name **AfuChat Technologies Ltd.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Install">
    Open the AfuChat listing and tap the **Install** button. The app (package `com.afuchat.afuapp`) will download and install automatically. You may be prompted to accept permissions during or after installation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open AfuChat">
    Once the installation completes, tap **Open** directly from the Play Store, or find the AfuChat icon on your home screen or app drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account or sign in">
    Follow the on-screen welcome flow to create a new account or sign in to an existing one. See [Create Your Account](/getting-started/create-account) for a full walkthrough.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Enable **automatic updates** in the Play Store settings for AfuChat so you always receive new features and security patches as soon as they are available. Go to the AfuChat Play Store page, tap the three-dot menu, and select **Enable auto-update**.
</Tip>

## Install via Direct APK Download

If the Google Play Store is unavailable in your region or on your device, you can download the AfuChat APK file directly from the official website.

<Warning>
  Only download the AfuChat APK from **afuchat.com**. APK files from third-party sites may be outdated, modified, or unsafe. AfuChat Technologies Ltd. is not responsible for any issues caused by unofficial APK sources.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable installation from unknown sources">
    On your Android device, go to **Settings → Security** (or **Settings → Apps & notifications → Special app access** on newer Android versions) and enable **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Visit afuchat.com on your device">
    Open your mobile browser and navigate to **https://afuchat.com**. Find the download section and tap **Download APK**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download and open the APK">
    Your browser will download the `.apk` file. Once the download completes, tap the notification or open the file from your Downloads folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the installation">
    Android will display an installation prompt showing the app name and permissions. Tap **Install** to proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch AfuChat">
    After installation finishes, tap **Open** to launch AfuChat and begin the account setup process.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  When you install AfuChat via APK, you will need to manually check for updates on afuchat.com periodically, or switch to the Google Play version to receive automatic updates going forward.
</Info>

## Sharing AfuChat with Friends

Every AfuChat profile and post generates a shareable link using AfuChat's universal link format (`https://afuchat.com/...`). You can also share the app download link directly — anyone who taps the link on an Android device will be taken straight to the Play Store listing.

AfuChat also supports **QR codes** for quick sharing. From inside the app, you can generate a personal QR code from your profile page. Others can scan it to visit your profile instantly — no typing required.

## Deep Links

Once AfuChat is installed on your device, any link using the `afuchat://` scheme or a `https://afuchat.com` universal link will open directly in the app rather than in a browser. This means:

- Tapping a shared post link jumps you straight to that post inside AfuChat.
- Tapping a profile link opens that person's AfuChat profile immediately.
- Notification links from email or SMS open the relevant screen inside the app.

<Tip>
  If a deep link opens in your browser instead of the app, check that AfuChat is set as the default handler for `afuchat.com` links. Go to **Settings → Apps → AfuChat → Open by default** and enable it.
</Tip>
